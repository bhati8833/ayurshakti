Part 23 - Oil Seeds (according to the Purāṇas)

Ikṣu (Sugarcane)[1] and oil-seeds also come under agricultural products. In the Purāṇas various types of oil-seeds are mentioned to be used in various rituals.

The most popular oil-seeds mentioned in ritual context are—

tila (sesame)[2],

sarṣapa (mustard)[3],

rājikā (a mustard-type seed)[4],

eraṇḍa (castor-seeds)[5] and

siddhārthaka (white mustard)[6] etc.

The sesame seeds are the most common of oil-seeds. They are considered deva-dhānya[7] i.e. divine grain but not ordinary grain. Hence at all rituals it seems to be important.

Moreover, [the following] the oil-seeds are also mentioned in the Purāṇas.—

kṣauma (linseeds)[8], and

akṣa (the seed of the bead of rosaries)[9]
