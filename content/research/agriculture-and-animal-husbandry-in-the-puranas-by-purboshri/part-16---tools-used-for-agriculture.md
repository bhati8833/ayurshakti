Part 16 - Tools Used for Agriculture

[Full title: Agriculture in the Modern Age of 20-21st Century (2) Tools Used for Agriculture]

It is established that the harvesting of the different crops is carried out at present only by physical work and the implements used are only normal sickles, the cutting edge of which is specially hollowed out for this reason.[1]

So the preliminary tillage may be carried out by following three methods—

(a) By the manual labour of digging the ground with hand devices,
(b) By ploughing with bullocks or other draught animals, and
(c) By mechanical power.[2]

Moreover, threshing machines, winnowing machines, feed cutting machines, stone threshing roller, wheat threshing roller etc. are also used. In modern times, tractors are being used for farming processes in India.

The scope for tillage with tractors and tractor-drawn implements lie mostly in three ways, viz.

(1) For clearing of scrub jungle or waste land to be newly brought under cultivation;
(2) For the ploughing and other tillage of land already under cultivation;
(3) For the hot weather deep ploughing of the black cotton soil.[3]

Indian farmers also are beginning to make great progress in the use of better implements. In many villages to-day we can see improved ploughs, hoes, water-lifts and other implements.[4] Likewise the use of plough is noticed in the Purāṇic days too. It is recommended in the Purāṇas that the ground for constructing a temple should be ploughed with white bullocks, that for a tank with bullocks of any colour, for preparing a garden and for digging well, black coloured bullocks are mentioned.[5] Hence, it can be known that the indigenous implements are still used today for the process of agriculture.
