## 📜 1. Section Overview & Classical Context

Agriculture and Animal husbandry in the Puranas

This page relates ‘Literature Review’ of the study on agriculture and animal husbandry in the Puranas—a category of Sanskrit literature describing the culture and heritage of ancient Indian society. Besides detailing animal care, the six chapters of this essay also documents various Puranic agricultural science: ploughing, sowing, soil-types/fertility tests, transplantation, manuring, irrigation/water-management, tanks, rainfall–Sun dependence, horticulture, plus crop lists (e.g., Vrihi, Yava, Godhuma) and festivals/rites.

Part 10 - Literature Review

The following books have been utilized as source materials for the study of Agriculture and Animal Husbandry-History of Agriculture in India(up to c. 1200 AD),published under the History of Science, Philosophy and Culture in Indian Civilization series, volume 5, part 1, edited by Lallanji Gopal & V.C. Kane’s History of Dharmaśāstra, volume 2, part 2 is also noteworthy. Mention must be made of Amarakoṣa of Amarasiṃha edited by Visvanath Jha, which helped to know the meaning of the words.

The Bhāgavata Purāṇa: Mytho-Social Study by S.S. Dange, Purāṇic Encyclopaedia by Vettam Mani, Vedic Mythological Tracts by R.N. The post-Purāṇic works which deal with the various aspects of agriculture and animal husbandry are the Kṛṣi Pārāśara of Parāśara, edited by Ramachandra Pandeya, Upavana-Vinoda of Śārṅgadhara edited by G.P. Majumder, Vṛkṣāyurveda of Surapāla edited by Gyanendra Pandey, Viśvavallabha of Chakrapāṇi Miśra edited by Nalini Sadhale, Kāśyapīyakṛṣisūkti of Kāśyapa edited by S.M. Ayachit, Bṛhatsaṃhitā of Varāhamihira edited by V.S. Sastri & M.R. Bhat, Śukranītisāra of Śukra edited by Sri Jivananda Vidyasagar Bhattacharyya and Arthaśāstra of Kauṭilya edited by R.Shamasastry The Cultural Heritage of India, volume 2 & 6, edited by S.Radhakrishnan has also contributed a lot to the field of agriculture and animal husbandry. For having the concept of agriculture and animal husbandry in the 20-21st Century, the books A History of Agriculture in India in four volumes by M.S. Randhawa, A Textbook on Ancient History of Indian Agriculture by R.C. Saxena, S.L. Choudhary, & Y.L. Nene and published by Asian Agri History Foundation, Agriculture and Animal Husbandry in India by M.S. Randhawa, Principles of Crop Husbandry by Y.K. Yegna Narayan Aiyer, A Handbook of Animal Husbandry and Dairying by V.T. Subbiah Mudaliar, Handbook of Animal Husbandry edited by R.R. Thakur are worth mentioning.
