Part 17 - Kingdoms Noted for Elephants and Kings Who Had Elephants

Anuparvatam situated between the Lauhitya and Sindhu is the forest noted for Padma class of elephants.[1] Kāverī, which takes its source on the Sahya hills, is noted for elephants in Drāviḍa deśa (Dakṣiṇa deśa).[2] Lauhitya (modern Brahmāputra) was noted for Padma class of elephants.[3] Utkala (at present it is a state of Orissa), a kingdom of Madhyadeśa, was noted for Vāmana elephants.[4] Aṅga(near modern Bhagalpur), an eastern kingdom,its king got war elephants from Devas.[5] King Bhoja had 21,000 elephants with silver and gold chains in east and west directions.[6] Kuvalayāpīḍa was the state elephant of Kaṃsa.[7] Bhagadatta was presented with elephants of Airāvata breeds by Kṛṣṇa.[8]

Elephants Used for Chariots:

Airāvata, the lord of elephants (Gajendra), which was a species of white elephants with four tusks and was the vehicle of Indra, travelled in the sun’s chariot during the month of Kārtika.[9] At the time of the churning of the ocean of milk, when the elephant Airāvata came out, Lord Indra took him and employed him as his vehicle.[10] Añjana, the elephant belonging to Saṃkīrṇa variety, was the vehicle of Yama, Supratika was the vehicle of Varuṇa,Padma was the vehicle of Kuvera and Vāmana was the vehicle of Agni.[11] Demon Nimi had a chariot of elephants.[12]
