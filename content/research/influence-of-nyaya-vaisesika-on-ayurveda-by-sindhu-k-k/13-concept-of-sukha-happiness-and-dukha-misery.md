13. Concept of Sukha (happiness) and Dukha (misery)

According to Vaisesika philosophy the concept of sukha and dukha as a result of the interaction between Atma, Indriya manas, and indriyarthas is sukha (Happiness) and Dukha are produced. (atmendriyamanortha santikart sukhadukhe ) 114 Accepting this Caraka says that, happiness and misery arise due to contact of the self, sense organ, mind and the sense 245

objects but when the mind is steadily concentrated to the self, both case to exist due to non-initiation and a supernatural power comes forth in the person. This state is known as yoga by the expert sages. 115 Cakrapani explain that the use or contact of senses etc. is the cause of happiness and misery and is proved as such by an 'anvayavyatirekha' (law of agreement in presence and absence) because, in spite of existence of senses and their object, if contact is here, effect is present otherwise not.
