15. Concept of God

Concept of God Kanada in his Vaisesika sutra has not envisaged any God. But later on, commentators like Prasastapada, accepted the existence of God. Effect of karma In the previous dispensations of life attached to Atma is called Adrstam (unknown) in Vaisesika philosophy. Kanada has attributed in it the initiation of activity. and there fore no God was envisaged. Prasastapada postulated that action is initiated due to desire of God. This adrstam is known as Daivam in Ayurveda. "nirdistam daivasabdena karma yat paurvadehikam  " 118 Moksa there is no such an aciton the effect of which is not to be suffer or enjoyed by the individual who does it. Diseases caused by such karmas are capable of nullifying the effect of treatment. They spontaneously subside when the effect of treatment. They spontaneously subside when the effects of karya are reduced as a result of sufferings. 249

Principles and theories postulated by Vaisesika darsana has been accepted by alk branches of science that developed in India.
