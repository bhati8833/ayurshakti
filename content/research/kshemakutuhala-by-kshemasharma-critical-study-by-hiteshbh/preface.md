poucements The critical study and editing of the Kshemakutuhala is mainly a study of the Pakasastra which was considered as a valuable and important knowledge in ancient times. The Kshema-kutuhala supplies very useful material of Dietology, Nutrition, Cookery and General Hygiene which are supposed to be important sciences even in modern times. The author has discussed these sciences in the light of Ayurvedic principles. But the Kshemakutuhala has not received proper attention which it really deserves. It was not even tried to prepare the critical edition of the Kshemakutuhala Hence I have taken the Kshemakutuhala to prepare its critical edition for the Ph.D.degree. An attempt is made to provide English equivalents for some of the technical Sanskrit Terms used in the Ayurveda, but wherever it is not possible to do so, original Sanskrit words have been maintained.
