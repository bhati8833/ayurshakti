## 📜 1. Section Overview & Classical Context

Charaka Samhita and Sushruta Samhita

This page relates ‘Preface’ of the study on the Charaka Samhita and the Sushruta Samhita, both important and authentic Sanskrit texts belonging to Ayurveda: the ancient Indian science of medicine and nature. The text anaylsis its medical and social aspects, and various topics such as diseases and health-care, the physician, their training and specialisation, interaction with society, educational training, etc.

Preface

I am immensely grateful to my supervisor, Dr. Susmita Basu Majumdar, for encouraging me to take up research on the history of Ancient Indian medicine. I consider myself very fortunate to be able to work with her and have her guidance, patience and support. The discussions we had were always stimulating.

I am indebted to my institution, South Calcutta Girls’ College for giving me permission to take leave under the UGC-FDP programme without which it would not have been possible to complete my research. I express my gratitude to the UGC for the study leave under the FDP programme.

The staff of all the libraries that I have used in the course of my work have been very helpful. I would also like to thank the National Library, Kolkata, the Asiatic Society, Kolkata, the Central Library, University of Kolkata and National Institute of Indian Medical Heritage, Hyderabad.

I am thankful to Dr. Manjula Devi, Prof. Dept. of Sanskrit, Gauhati University, Ms. Priyanka Dalal, research scholar, Dept. of Sanskrit, Jadavpur University and Ms. Smita Halder, research scholar, Dept. of Ancient Indian History and Culture, Calcutta University for their help.

I am truly grateful to all my family members and friends for their constant support and motivation. My thanks go to my parents who have always encouraged me to explore my potential. I am thankful to my parents-in-law for their support. I would especially like to thank my husband, Sanjoy, who has been a pillar of support. I am particularly grateful to him for helping out with the proof-reading.

Nayana Sharma
