Study of Avarta Marma

Avarta Etymology—Derived from the word Aan + vrith + Ghanh which means whirlpool. Aan and Ghanh are upasarga and pratyaya respectively. Vrith has the meaning of existence and that which is repeated. Description The situation of this marma is on the surface of the frontal lobe of the brain underneath the groove above the super ciliary arch on the both sides corresponding to the articulation of the posterior border of the orbital plate with a small wing in the sphenoidal bone. Injury to this marma causes impaired vision or blindness. This is due to injury to the visual centres on the surface of brain. They are two in number coming under Sandhi marma group. The prognosis is vaikalyakara in nature. It is half anguli in extent.Its bhuta structure is Jala. Adhidevata is Isvara. Surface Anatomy These can be located on the lateral one third of the eye brow.
