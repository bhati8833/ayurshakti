Study of Utkshepa Marma

Utkshepa Marmam—This is derived from Kship dhatu which is prefixed by Ud = Urdhvam Ksipathi. That which lifts up. Description The situation of this marma is above the Sankha and near the hairy margin on the scalp on both sides of head. Injury to this marma by a foreign body keeps the person alive as long as it is kept within the wound or it falls down on its own accord, after the formation of the tissue which acts as a barrier between the foreign body and the injured tissue of the body. But if it is drawn out immediately after the injury, the person dies at once due to bleeding. (sudden loss of nervous energy prana.) They are two in number and its prognosis is Vishalyaghna in nature. Its ashraya is snayu Its bhuta structure is Vatha. Adhidevata is Brahma 132

Surface Anatomy The marma can be located diagonally above the temporal region slightly inside the hairs towards the parietal part of the skull. It falls almost on Parotid gland Posterior auricular nerve Main trunk of facial nerve emerging from stylomastoid foramen Nerve to posterior belly of digastric muscle and to stylohyoid muscle Temporal branches Zygomatic branches Parotid duct Buccal branches Marginal mandibular branch Cervical branch Plate No. 9 The anatomical area of Utkshepa the vertical line above the ear. Underneath lies the temporalis tendons and below the skull is intracranial cavity, and the venous sinuses the wall of which are made of covering layers of the brain. Anatomical Structures corresponding to this marma ☐ Temporal fascia ☐ Parietal branch of superficial temporal artery Zygomatic temporal nerve ☐ Temporalis muscle 133

Discussion If the foreign body/ dart is extracted immediately, the above artery bleeds heavily leading to death. If the granulation tissues is formed and bleeding is arrested and the patient survives. even if the dart is removed. Tamil varma sastra Poikai life centre is located 5cms. above the curvature near the starting point of the ears. If Poikai life-centre is affected seriously, the patient will faint instantaneously. All the five senses will go out of control. There will be darkness in vision. Tiredness will set in. Frothing will appear in the mouth. The mouth will open widely, while the head will be tilting upwards. The The patient who will experience severe pain in the nerves will rise up all of a sudden. Within 12 hours, the patient should be treated for cure. 134
