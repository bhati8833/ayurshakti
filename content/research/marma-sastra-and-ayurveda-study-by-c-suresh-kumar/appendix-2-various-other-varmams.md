Appendix 2: Various other Varmams

It may also be mentioned that the Tamil varma sastra speaks of certain other marma situated in the anatomical area once discussed above they are discussed below. 184

Yenthi varmam is positioned 5 cms. Below the Thivalai varmam on either side of the rib cage. The Yenthi varmam located on the left side is known as left yenthi varmam and the one on the right side yenthi varmam. This has got this name because with this point we can hold the whole body. The victim of this varmam disorder will suffer form tetanus. The patient will becomes weak and lose his hearing power. Within 3 hours the patient should be attended to, for cure. Kaikkoottu varmam is situated at the opposite side (back side) of the Yenthi varmam. If Kaikkoottu varmam is affected, then the ball and the socket of the shoulder joint will be affected. The patient will faint and his hands will be raised. The tongue will stammer and tetanus will set in. The patient should be treated within four hours of disorder of this life-centre. Kutthu varmam, Ulkutthu varmam, Pulpatru varmam, Kurukku varmam, all these four life centres (varmams) points are situated on the back. Thoosi varmam, Thoosika varmam, Thudi varmam, Pidi varmam (Idi varmam), Kanal 185

varmam (Kazhalai varmam), Valai varmam, Eelai varmam (Kulai varmam), Vil varmam, Hanumaar varmam, all the above nine life centres are situated around the nipples of both the breasts. In males these varma points are easy to locate but in female they cannot be located easily. These life centres are situated above the lungs and hence are very sensitive. The lungs will get affected once these life-centres are inflicted. Fainting, blood vomiting, thirst, constipation and retention of urine are observed in patient who has disorders of this life-centre. Occurrence of tuberculosis and hunch-back are other symptoms. The limbs of the patients will also get affected. The patient who is affected with the impact of these life-centres is to be attended within three hours, failing which death due to blood vomiting will occur. Ari varmam point is situated two inches below the Koombu varmam. The damage to Ari varmam will lead to reddening of the eyes, fainting and tetanus and the patient will coil himself into a bundle and will gasp and shout in pain. 186

The patient has to be treated within seven hours and thirty minutes of the varma disorder. Mundellu varmam is situated on the floating ribs on both sides. In case Mundellu life-centre is inflicted, the intestine will get affected. The patient will have diarrhoea, fever and shivering cough and fainting are also likely to occur. Saruthi varmam is of two types. One is Munsaruthi varmam and the other Pinsaruthi varmam. The Munsaruthi varmam is positioned 7 cms. Above the Mundellu varmam. Pin saruthi varmam is situated 2 cms. above Munsaruthi varmam. These two life centres are found on either side of the body. If these life centres are disturbed, tetanus, stomach pain, cramps, and swelling of the body will occur. Pain and breathing difficulties will also be experienced. Below the waist shivering will occur. Numbness of legs will set in. Pallai varmam is situated on either side of the umbilicus. The affected person will bend. Air (vayu) will rise up. The victim will experience giddiness. 187

The patient should be attended to within seven hours of the occurance of impact to this lifecentre. Surukku varmam resides adjacent to Mundellu varmam. Both motion and urine will be retained once surukku varmam is disturbed. Pain in the legs will occur. In course of time, other sufferings will arise. Kareeral varmam life-centre resides in the liver (kareeral). Owing to this varma disorder, the patient's radial artery below the thumb will be pronounced in appearance. The patient will be restless, and his body will stretch, and in due course bend like a bow. Further this disorder reflects the signs and symptoms of Thoosika varmam. The patient should be treated within three hours of the varma disorder. Kaikkatti varmam life centre is situated 8 cms. Below the Thummi varmam. This is situated near adaipu varma on the front of the shoulder. This is termed as Allumel Adaipu Kaikoottel in Varma kannadi. Odimurivu (32) calls it Varma mada Adappathil Kaikettu and located at the shoulder joint. 188

Brahmma varmam is otherwise named as Annakkala varmam or Undhi varmam or Aamendra varmam or Ayan varmam. If Brahma varmam is affected, the patient will have running nose giddiness, writhing of the body, weakness in the limbs and closing of eyes. The body will become reddish and the patient will bleed through the anus and mouth. Seminal discharge will be observed and the stomach will bloat. The patient will have much problem within seven hours of being affected. The patient should be treated within one hour. Valampuri varmam is situated 2. 5 cms. on the right side of the penis. Damage to Valampuri varmam leads to retention of urine of urine and motion, stomach upset, numbness in the limbs and pricking pain in the penis. Within seven hours of the impact of this life-centre, the patient should be treated. Idampuri varmam is located 2. 5 cms. on the left side of the penis. Damage to Idampuri varmam shows the same signs and symptoms observed for Valampuri varmam. In addition, pain in the penis and body-ache will occur. 189

Villurumi varmam is situated near the Valampuri varmam. If Villrumi life centre (varmam) is affected due to any impact. The lower part of the abdomen becomes numb. The victim will become weak. Body pain, giddiness, fever and pain in the lower part of the abdomen and mental confusion will also occur. Vallurumi varmam resides near idampuri varma point. If affected by impact at Vallurumi varmam, the signs and symptoms will generally be the same as that of the Vallurumi varmam. The disorder of Vallurumi varmam should be treated within seven hours. Narankukutthi varmam is located in the groin between the genital region and the anus. If affected the patient back will have giddiness, breathlessness, loss of speech and swelling of the body. If the damage is severe, patient will die. Ani varmam which is also called Melani varmam. Ani varmam is situated 10 cms, above the Narankukutthi varmam, 2.5 cms. above the male genital organ. 190

Involuntary discharge of semen will be the symptom of this varma disorder. Anthai varmam is Location d 2.5 cms. above the Melani varmam. If this varmam is damaged, the patient will become unconscious quite often. Like this, the victim will suffer. Swasam adappan is seen in the mid axillary line at the level of the two nipples. If injured the patient patient complaints stretching sensation of the nerves fainting frothy discharge from the mouth. Nooluvalippan is situated three and a half inches above the nipples. On injury lead to congestion of chest with phlegm. Froth from the mouth, stiffness in both hands. This ewill lead to Rajayakshma. Urulumani marma are seen even below the malapandam near the testicles. If injured this will cause fainting and the two testicles ascends towards the nabhi and get itself lodged there. Give pressure over two angula below the anal region and also gently press over the navel and bring these testicles downwards. 191
