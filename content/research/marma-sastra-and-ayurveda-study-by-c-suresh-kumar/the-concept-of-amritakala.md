The Concept of Amritakala

The concept of Amritakala, is one of the peculiar theories of Varma chikitsa [marma-chikitsa]. It might be very difficult to understand this with a rational mind. Here just like the moon playing a very important role during the high tide and low tide, it is said that it plays a very important role in human body. That might be reason for the aggravation of certain disease during certain seasons. There are thirty locations all over the body where the amritakala travels depending upon the waxing and waning of the moon in the sky.
