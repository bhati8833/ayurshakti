Study of Indravasti Marma

Etymology of Indravasti (Indravasthi / Indrabasti / Indrabasthi)—This as a single word was not available for reference. Indra means lord of gods. Supreme. Vasti refer vasti marma. 243

Description An injury to the marma which is situated in the middle muscle of the calf to the distance of between twelve and thirteen fingers width from the ankle, is known as Indravasti which results in excessive haemorrhage and ends in death. The situation of marma is above the wrist and in the middle of forearm. Injury to this marma endangers life by diminished blood supply. Bhuta structure agni and Soma property. (P.V. Krishna Rao). Adhidevata is Brahma. Surface Anatomy Locating Indravasti is easier on the legs. It is exactly on the tip of the calf muscle body (gastrocnemius) from where two of its tendons emerge forming a V angle. It is better palpable with patient sitting straight and semiflexed legs. To locate Indravasti on the fore hand, draw an imaginary line from the mid point of cubital fossa to the mid point of the wrist joint on ventral part of the hand. Discussion Basti used here probably refer to the The region of the marma organ urinary bladder. particularly in legs in surrounded with morphological structures resembling the conical shape of Basti. The body of gastrocnemius when contracted assumes a conical shape and the sudden tapering of tendons may give an impression of urinary bladder attached to the urethral tube. To endanger life due to an injury to this marma is due to the fact that there is plenty of place here where the extravasated blood can collect. Probably that is the reason this marma is termed as Indravasthy a better vessel. As the blood only gets collected inside the body the patient dies due to internal bleeding. In the preface of Rasayoga Sagar it is mentioned that Indravasti is popliteal fossa. As per the discussion available from Susruta, that cannot be true.
