Study of Lohitaksha Marma

Lohitaksha Etymology □ A red eye, □ Indian cuckoo, □ A kind of snake, an epiphet of lord Vishnu.. 258

Description An injury to the Lohitaksha marma situated respectively a little above the Urvi marma and below the Vamkshana (Groin) and placed near the thigh, will lead to excessive haemorrhage and cause paralysis. (of the leg). Situation of the marma is above the bahvee marmam and below the auxillary fold and at the root of the upper extremity - Brachium. Injury to this marma lead to paralysis of the whole extremity due to diminished blood supply. In lower limb the situation of this marma is above urvi marma and below groin at the root of thigh. (P.V. Krishna Rao) Its ashraya is in sira group. Prognosis is vaikalyakara and half anguli extent. Its butha structure is saumya. Adhidevata is Narayana. Surface Anatomy This marma is very easy to locate at the top of the thigh. It lies near the great inguinal canal through which the femoral vessels emerge from abdominal cavity. The patient must be on supine position and preferably the under garments (slips) removed. The transverse elastic bands of the slips may cover over the point of this marma and hinder proper palpation. On the hands it is not easier for palpation as on thighs. The examiner has the to probe little 259

towards the axillary area on the median surface of upper arm. It is desirable that the arm should not be too much abducted.
