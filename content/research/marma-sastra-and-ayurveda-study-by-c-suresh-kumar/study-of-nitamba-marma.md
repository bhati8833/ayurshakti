Study of Nitamba Marma

Nitamba Etymology—The word nitamba is derived from tamu dhatu. This prefixed by ni pratyaya. Tamu means Glani and also Kamksha. As after sexual act the part which is tired first is Nitambha. In the second context it is said that the males derive urge for sex after seeing this part. Description Situation of the marma is above the sroneekandam (pelvic girdle) and both the sides of the vertebral column. Injury to this marma leads to the atrophy of the lower limb and endangers life in course of time. They are two in number, these are asthi marmas and also Kalanthara pranahara. It is half anguli in extent. Its bhuta structure is Agni and Jala. Adhidevatha is Raudra. Surface Anatomy The examination of this marma is preferably done in patients lying on chest and abdomen. The nitamba marma lies on half the distance between sacral bone and the greater trochanter of 198
