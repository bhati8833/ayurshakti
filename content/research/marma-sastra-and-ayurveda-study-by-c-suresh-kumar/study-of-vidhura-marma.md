Study of Vidhura Marma

Vidhura Etymology—The word Vidhura is derived from the root Dhura = weight. This word is formed by Vigatha Dhura = Chesta nashta, or imbalance of the body. Description The situation of this marma is behind the pinna of the ear. It is seen near the pit like structure seen behind the ear. They are two in number. Its prognosis is Vaikalyakara. They are half anguli in parimana. If injured will lead to total deafness. Its bhuta structure is Jala. Adhidevata is Isvara. 6,7,8 Vaghbata consider this as Dhamani marma and Susruta consider this as Snayu marma. As this lead to deafness Susruta's opinion of this marma as a snayu marma stands good. Surface Anatomy It is easy to palpate marma behind the pinna of ear just below the mastoid bone where the ligament of strenocliedo mastoid muscle joins it. It fall exactly in the groove between mastoid bone and the mandibles. This point almost corresponds to tympanic membrane which lies immediately vulnerable 116
