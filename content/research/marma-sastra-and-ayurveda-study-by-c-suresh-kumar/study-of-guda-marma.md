Study of Guda Marma

The word Guda is derived from the root Gu which means Sabda and also Gu Pureeshotsarge. The

adhidevatha of the organ Guda is Mithran and some opine this as Rudra. Description Situation of this marma is in the anorectal region, where through which the flatus and faeces are expelled. Injury to this marma is fatal. This is a mamsa marma according to Susruta, and Dhamani marma as per Vaghbata. It is sadya pranaharam and is four anguli in extent. Its bhuta structure is Teja Surface Anatomy Thus Guda marma is superficially referred to region surrounding the anus.
