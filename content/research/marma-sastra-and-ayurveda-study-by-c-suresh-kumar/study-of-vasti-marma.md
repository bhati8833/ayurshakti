Etymology of Vasti (Vasthi, Basti, Basthi)—"Vas = The term Vasthi is derived from the root Nivasey Nabheradah. The meaning of which is the place where the urine is collected. Description The situation of the marma is within the pelvis corresponding to bladder, the wall is fibroelastic with a few plain muscle fibres and blood vessels. 159

Injury to this marma, as a whole endangers life immediately. This may be due to the complications or to shock by stimulation of the vesical, the hypogastric nerve plexus and the nerves in the wall of the bladder. There is also danger to life if a stone in the bladder injures its wall on both the sides, but when the lesion is confined to one side, extravasation of urine may Body of bladder Neck of bladder Uvula of bladder Prostate and prostatic urethra Urethral bulb Peritoneum Fundus of bladder Body of bladder Opening of left ureter Interureteric fold Trigone of bladder Inferior pubic ramus Crus of penis and ischiocavernosus muscle Superficial perineal (Colles') fascia Plate No. 15: The anatomical area of Vasti occur into pelvic cellular tissues which can be treated with great effort. This is a sadya pranahara marma and is four anguli in extent. Vaghbata describes the shape of Vasti (bladder) to be Danurvakram (curved like a bow) and having one outlet in the lower part. Its bhuta structure is Agni. Adhidevatha is Rudra. 160

According to Charaka, Vasti, which is the receptacle of urine, is situated amidst Stoola Guda (large intestines and rectum), Mushka (testes), Sevanee (phrenum) and the ducts that carry semen and urine. The bladder is to all the water bearing ducts, as is the sea to all the stream. Injury to this marma may cause obstruction to vata, mutra and mala, pain in the groin, penis and bladder. Surface Anatomy 7 It can be felt on deep palpation of the lower abdomen. Though Vasti marma is located deeper, the abdominal wall (Vasti the relative area on the Pradesha) shall represent the disturbances in the marma. Therfore, both superficial and deep inspection of Vasti marma is advisable. The Ari varmam is another eight fingers below the Rudhira varmam. The Rudhira varmam is located near the heart. If injured death occurs within eight hours. Symptoms like running nose, fainting, loss of bladder control are seen. Kulayana marma Jalapandam The area of this marma is three angula above the navel and four angula below the Thila kuzhi marma.
