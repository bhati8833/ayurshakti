Study of Bahvi or Urvi Marma

Etymology of Bahvi / Urvi—Bahu madhye Bahvi. Uru Madhye Urvi, Uru is derived from the word Urn which means Achadane That which covers. Description A perforation of Urvi marma situated in the middle of the uru (thigh) results in the atrophy of the muscle of the leg, owing to the incidental haemorrhage. The situation of this marma is in the middle of the forearm. Injury brings atrophy due to diminished blood supply (P.V. Krishna Rao). The bhuta predominance is soumya. This is a sira marma, vaikalyakara in nature and half anguli in extent.
