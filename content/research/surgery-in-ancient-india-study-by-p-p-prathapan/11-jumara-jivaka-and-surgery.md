11. Jumara Jivaka and Surgery

Kumara Jivaka, son of King Bimbisara of Maghada kingdom, India, and a courtesan, was a physician, surgeon and pediatrician contemporary to Buddha. He studied medicine in Taxila and performed cranial and other major operations mentioned in the history of Indian medicine. He had learned this art for seven years from master Atreya who was the crown physician of the Ayurvedic physicians at that time. The young Kumara Jivaka was also crowned three times King of the physicians because of his extra ordinary cures performed on his father, queen step mother and others.
