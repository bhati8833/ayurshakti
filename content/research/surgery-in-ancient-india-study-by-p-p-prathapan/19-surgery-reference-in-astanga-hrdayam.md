19. Surgery Reference in Astanga Hrdayam

Astanga-Hridaya of Vagbhata is the most popular work till today through out India. Later several compilations appeared and became popular in certain parts of the country among practitioners. As well as known, Caraka Samhita gives stress on general medicine while Susruta Samhita gives importance to surgical aspects. In contrast Astanga Hrdayam, as the name suggests, deals with all the eight branches to the required extent for a successful physician and surgeon. Astanga Samgraha, written by an Ayurveda acharya named Vagbhata is also the compilation of the works by both Charaka Samhita and Susruta Samhita. Astanga Hrdaya deals surgery in many places.
