14. Kautilya’s Arthasastra and Surgery

Kautilya, the minister of the emperor Chandragupta Maurya, wrote Arthasastra in the fourth century B.C. It is a valuable document on cultural data which also includes and role of the status of 62 physicians and medicine. According to Arthasastra physicians are classified in to five types. They had expert military surgeons. This suggests that the practice of surgery also existed in those days. Error of treatment causing death was severely punished. A surgeon had to lose a limb, for causing a patient to lose it by his mistaken operation.
