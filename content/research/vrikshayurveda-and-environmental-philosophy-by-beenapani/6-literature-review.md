6. Literature review

The research work on environmental philosophy in Vrksayurveda texts has got its limited scope in the arena of the research publications. A wide prescription of the aspects of Vrikshayurveda is rarely available both in terms of available materials on

23 the same subject as well as in terms of various other Sanskrit texts covering the traits of Vrikshayurveda in its parts. The materials therefore so collected and reviewed herein is quite spectacular and well evident to reflect scarcity of the subjective concern. However, glossaries of the works done on the Vrikshayurveda are as follows:Though several texts both primary and secondary are available on Vrikshayurveda still the analysis of their contents especially from the angle of environmental philosophy has not been stressed upon as a special treatise. But some articles and writings available through internet lay stress on the philosophy of environment. Contemporary environmental problems are completed as they involve amuse of attitude towards nature and ethical obligations. The social, religious, psychological and scientific explanations of the relationship between man and nature are some of the crucial aspects which are discussed in the texts on Vrikshayurveda here and there but not systematically as a significant special study. The present study of environmental philosophy demands increased interdisciplinary cooperation between philosophers, scientists, religious experts and experts in the field of Sanskrit literature to highlight the importance of integrated relation between man and nature. Such as crossdepartmental approach will be consistent with the contemporary

24 trend towards making philosophy more empirical. Present concerns about climate change, loss of some species and environmental degradation lead to a further integration of the work of thinkers in the field of literature, science, and philosophy. As long as scientific facts about the environment pose ethical and philosophical quandaries for philosophers, it is likely that many interdisciplinary research agenda will emerge. Some of the scanty works which put stress upon the environmental philosophy may be enumerated as follows - 1. Proceedings of the natural seminar on environmental awareness reflected in Sanskrit literature, edited by V. N. Jha, Centre of Advanced Study in Sanskrit, University of Poona, 1991. There are forty essays in this compilation. The articles have been arranged to bring to the surface the outlook of ancient Indians towards nature and everything that is incorporated in the surroundings of man with the sole aim to derive in sight from such outlooks which can benefit the modern society and modern man. Often it is said that the development of science and technology should go hand in hand with the balance of human values. Thus, it was thought that it would not be a bad idea to peep into the past to derive some insights which might help the modern man without

25 coming on the way of scientific and technological developments. The present volume does not claim to be an exhaustive survey of environmental awareness reflected in Sanskrit literature but it is expected to serve merely as a specimen of the magnitude of thinking about the relationship between man and his surroundings, ultimately aiming at the peace and balance that a man is desirous of. 2. Vanaspati (Plants and Plant Life as in Indian Treaties and Traditions) by Girija Prasanna Majumdar, Published by the University of Calcutta, 1927 The researcher has taken a vivid attempt putting forth the information's, classifying them and systematically arranging the aspects of contemplations of the ancients on plants and plant life which proceeded in India. Further, the book encountered its reference by means of three book references viz. Book I - Botany and Philosophic Speculations; Book II Botany and Science of Medicine; Book III - Botany and Science of Agriculture. 3. Different perspective of Environmental Ethics by Dr. Pratima Ganguli & Dr. Mritunjay Kumar, published by Shivalik Prakashan, Delhi, 2008 The author aptly attempted on an exploration upon the values in environment and also focused a few important reasons of the

26 moral dilemma generally arise in this field. The book is divided into eight chapters wherein chapter VI & VII do have some subchapters. However, a conscious effort reflects in its subjects to cover all the aspects of environmental ethics to a large extent. 4. Asvattha in Everyday Life as related in Puranas by Purna Chandra Ojha, Ashish Singhal for Sundeep Prakashan, New Delhi, 1991 This book annunciates the role of asvasttha in day today human life. It further reflects with several examples about the cutting of asvasttha tree or the incision of its leaves has been considered to be a great sacrilege. Though the scope of the research work of this book is limited only to Puranas, still exhaustive attempts have been made in this book to show the evolution of the tradition of beliefs and faiths associated with this tree from the prehistoric age to the modern age. 5. Sayana's Subhasita Sudhanidhi, By K. Krishnamoorthy, Karnataka University, Dharwar, 1968 This book is concedered to be the first anthology in Sanskrit literature arranging quotations from Kavyas and Sastras under the four recognized values of life, viz. dharma, artha, Kama and moksa.
