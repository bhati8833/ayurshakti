4. The Synonyms of Trees in Sanskrit

In Sanskrit literature the word vrksa gives the impression of a punlinga sabda the outcome of which is in the form of brasca + ksa. It is in accordance to the founders of namalinganusasana or amarakosa tikakara as to the formation of the word vrksa have presented the following verse. 14. Rgveda, VIII.48.3.

17 "vrksati vrksa varane pacadyac vrsracyate va okasca chedane ak'|| The meaning of which reads as - (Amarakosa, 2nd kanda, Vanausadhi barga,) The one who undertakes to serve or the one who has utilized the growth of a thing or a flower or the one which got a cut through the help of other is thus termed as vrksa. In Sanskrit text various synonyms of the trees are found which are very significant in highlighting the benevolence and characters of the trees. Those are found in some books such as Amarakosa, visvakosa, etc. the names of the trees are such as mahiruha, vrksa, sakhi, vitapi, padapa, taru, anokah,kuta, sala, palasi, druh, druma, agama, etc. vrkso mahiruha sakho vitapi padapastaruh/ anokahah kutah salah palasi drudrumagamah // (Amarakosa, 2nd kanda, Vanausadhi barga, v.5) However, in the context of environmental awareness the meaning of vrksa whereby it is said for the one who vitalizes the growth of a thing or a flower appears to give a more appropriate meaning.
