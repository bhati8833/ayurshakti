6. Environmental Philosophy behind the Nourishment of Trees

Surapala recommends the philosophy beyond the growth of the young trees to be administered every seventh or eighth day with small quantity of fish, flesh and sesamum cocked together (v.107). Young trees are to be protected from heat, strong wind flow, smoke, fire, and spider (v.108). He also mentions the rules for watering plants (v.109, 110, 111, 112) according to the types of land. The writer here beautifully justifies the extreme satisfaction of the trees they get from their fruits and flowers when they are set free from the suspicion of any impurity and are subjected to treatment (v.116). He recommends several methods for protecting the plants concerning their nourishment techniques. These include besmearing, watering, sprinkling, and fumigation and sometimes combining them even. Surapala lays down separate prescriptions for separate plants individually (v.122 -157).

240 VI.5. Environmental Philosophy behind the Care and Treatment of Trees against Diseases: VI.5.1. Plant Disorders: Surapala had given a vivid exposition on the knowledge of plant diseases with very originality and the sense of interesting expressions. He classifies the diseases broadly into two categories; those arising from outside and the one from their body within (v.165). The internal diseases are caused by the disorders of wind (vata), phlegm (kapha), and bile (pitta) whereas, those affecting from outside are caused by lacking in vermin's, frosts, etc. (v.166). VI.5.2. The Treatment of Plant Diseases: The scribe of the text has prescribed very systematically and. methodically the treatment for all plant diseases. In comparison just like human body plants to suffer from problems such as displacement or dislocation of flower, trunk, leaves, barks and the author has also mentioned the for its care and treatment as well. The diseases due to phlegm can be cured by the application of oil cakes of white mustard to the roots of the tree and irrigate thereof with water in which sesamum has been dissolved (v. 188). The diseases due to wind can be cured by digging out the earth

241 near their roots (v. 189) and the diseases due to bile can be removed by sprinkling water mixed with milk honey and the decoction of madhuka (v.191) and further the trees are relived of diseases caused by bile when irrigated with the decocted water of the fruits of three myrobalan (triphala) (v. 192). The author however has mentioned systematically different treatments for various other types of plant disorders too.
