8. Environmental Values reflected in Vishvavallabha

The Vishvavallabha [Vishwa Vallabha] text of Chakrapani Mishra also describes the protection and care of plants in his Chapter VI, Verses 1-9, wherein he mentions that storm, frost, fire, insects, cobwebs and rats are danger to the trees. So, one must strive hard to protect the trees from them by fixing the sticks around the plant to support it, as such, there would be no danger to it from strong wind. By selecting a proper site for the garden, safe from fire, trees can be guarded against the danger of smoke. Trees planted in designs of mandapa etc. should be vigilantly protected from fire. In grassy lands they should be protected from destructive fire. Chakrapani 83 Kautilya Arthasastra, 3.19, P. 247

A wise planter should treat the roots with powdered sesame and sprinkle ashes over it. Cobwebs can be removed by cloth or grass fastened to an end of a stick or by a feather of a peacock. Clever planter should protect all trees by constructing a temporary shade, or by fixing thorny plants, or by digging trenches or by some other devices. 84 (Ch. VI, Vv. 7, 8, Vishvavallabha )
