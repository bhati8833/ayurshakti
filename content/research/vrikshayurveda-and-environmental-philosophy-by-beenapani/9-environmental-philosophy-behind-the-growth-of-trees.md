9. Environmental Philosophy behind the Growth of Trees

243 VI.8. Environmental Philosophy behind the Growth of Trees as influenced by the Implantation of certain other types of Trees: The rationality behind the growth of trees influencing the favourable yield of other crops is in other way called as plant indicators for crops. Therefore, the author indicates the presence of nyagrodha to be fir for yielding barley crop, tinduka being good for svastika and asvattha tree being favorable for all type of crops (v.320). Likewise, jambu tree is favorable for the growth of sesame and black gram, sirisa tree fit for green gram, madhuka being favorable for the production of wheat and saptaparna fit for yielding barley crop (v.321). These prescriptions prove the indigenous knowledge of farming.
