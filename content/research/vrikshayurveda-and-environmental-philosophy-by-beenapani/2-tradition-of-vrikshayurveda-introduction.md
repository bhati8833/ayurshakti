0. 1.8. Tradition of Vrksayurveda texts in Sanskrit literature: The term Vrikshayurveda which means plant science or the science of plants is to be divided as vrksayuh and Veda. It is the Veda which refers science of plant life or longevity of plants (vrksayuh). Vrikshayurveda is thus the science of rearing of plants. In other words it is the science that resulted from the application of ayurveda to the phenomena of plants.
