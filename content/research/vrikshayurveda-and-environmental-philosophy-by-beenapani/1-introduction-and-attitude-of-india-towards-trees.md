Introduction 1 0. 1. 1. Biogenetic resource of India: India has forever been glorified for its rich and vivid biogenetic resource. The abounding heritage of our flora has made it an incredible nation worldwide. The bounty of its richness is well prevalent in the sylvan civilization. Moreover, a remarkable awareness and knowledge of its varied flora with diverse uses has been shown traditionally. Trees have always been the friends to and humanbeing have lived amidst them from time immemorial. Trees as such have been held in regard with deep respect and were reverence on account of sanctity since ancient times. Our ancestors adore and idolize trees and forest so much that they even regarded and worshiped them as abodes of vrksadevata and vanadevata. There are voluminous examples of tree groves and flower garden in ancient India. Literally, these groves of tree and gardens were termed as vrksavatikas and puspavatikas. We can find ample of such descriptions of floral culture in different Sanskrit literature where the plantation of trees was considered as sacred and pure. yasyam vrksa vanaspatya dhrvastisthanti visvaha/ prthivim visvadhayasam dhrtamacchavadamasi // (A.V.XII.1.27)
