149 III.1. Environmental Values reflected in Surapala's Vrksayurveda:—Vrikshayurveda by Surapala thus gives an ample reverence to the subject as mentioned in its verses content wise. The author has beautifully arranged the sequence of the subject references from latent to potent. However, the reflection of the environmental values found in Vrikshayurveda may be reflected through the following aspects:III.1.1. The Importance of Trees and Plants: The 1st chapter of the core text, Vrikshayurveda beginning with the glorification and the importance of trees consists of 19 verses ranging from verse 4 - 23. A condensed review can be taken to exemplify the sense of such importance of trees and plants. The author however instructed us to labour hard to protect the trees and take care of them. The trees help us greatly through their shade, flowers and fruits in pursuit of dharma, artha, kama. So plantation of trees is a religious task. In verse no. 4 of Vrikshayurveda, Surapala compared the trees with sons. If the sons of a father do not serve the purpose of Dharma or Artha being wayward and capricious, they are to be treated inferior as that of the trees of the forest.

With man's growing familiarity with the trees, the services and benefits he received from them, the importance of the trees grew. In Vedic period they were treated as God and people worship him as "Tree God". The life of a tree is totally dedicated for the service of mankind and animal kingdom. So, importances of the trees are so great, both in ancient, medieval and modern period. We have studied that plantation of trees solves all the problems of physical environment in which we are living. At the same time plantation of trees gives us the means and methods to attain dharma, artha, kama and moksa. As Surapala the author of Vrikshayurveda has mentioned this idea in verse no. 8 of his text as follows:ae tat satyam prijnaya vrksaropam samarabhet / dharmarthakamamoksanam drumebhyah sadhanam yatah // (Vrikshayurveda Ch.1, V. 8) In Varah Purana, Ch..176, also it is mentioned that growing of orchids and flower gardens is a noble did and one who does so attain dharma. By performing such one can gain surprisingly good results.
