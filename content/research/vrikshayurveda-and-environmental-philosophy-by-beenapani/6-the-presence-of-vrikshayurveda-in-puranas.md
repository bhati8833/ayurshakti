6. The presence of Vrikshayurveda in Puranas

Saint and sages live in the lap of nature surrounded by deep forests. They feel the economic utility of trees and plants and various forests products for their religious activities. They know that forests sustain their lives. So for their proper protection, they lay down certain rules to encourage the plantation of trees. They always give emphasis for the growth of vegetations world and maintenance of proper balance. In some of the Puranas we find verses relating to trees, gardens in the context of many topics of social and religious significance. The Puranas which contains chapters on Vrikshayurveda, are Agnipurana and Visnudharmottara-purana .

120 II.3.1. Vrksayurveda Text found in Agni Purana: The chapter (282) of the Agnipurana contains fourteen verses about the science of the treatment of plants. It is presented in the form of a narration by Dhanvantari. He mentions the importance and merits of plants planted in different directions of the house. Plaksa to the north, vata to the east, mango to the south and asvattha to the west is auspicious (v.1). The thorny trees to be grown on the southern side near the house are auspicious. In a house of residence a garden is to be planned. The planting of trees is to be done after performing religious rites and worshipping the Brahamana and the moon. The five fixed asterism vayu, hasta, prajapati, Visnu and mula are considered auspicious for planting trees (V.3-4). For watering of the trees a reservoir is to be made in the garden. After worshipping Varuna, Visnu and rains (parjanya) that digging of the water reservoir is to be done (V-5-6a). Planted trees are to be irrigated at the end of summer both in the evening and morning, on alternate days in winter and in the night in rainy season (V-7b-8a). Arista, asoka, Punnaga, sirisa, Jambu, Bakula, pomegranate and plantain are to be planted in the garden. A proper distance of twenty cubits between plants is the best and 16 cubits is middling

121 and 12 cubits is the worst. If planted such trees are to be transferred from one place to another. The closely grown trees become fruitless. The affected parts of trees are to be cleared with the help of a weapon (knife) (V-9b). A paste of vidanga and ghee is to be smeared and it is to be irrigated with cold water. In the case of premature destruction of fruits the tree are to be sprinkled with water mixed with kulattha, masa, mudga, Barley and sesame (V- 10). For preparing a decoction for sprinkling to be effective for all kinds of trees, it requires powder of the stool of goats and sheep, barley and sesamum. Irrigating with washing of fish water causes the growth of trees. The meat of fish covered with vidanga and rice is the pregnancy desire of the trees (V-13). This destroys the diseases of all the trees without exception. The Agnipurana depicts the chapters very briefly and it is based on the two texts of Brihat-vamhita and Visnudharmottara-purana II.3.2. The narrator says not to plant thorny trees near the house rather to avoid them always (V-2). The garden is to be planned on the left side of the house. To improve the quality of the soil one should first sow there sesamum and when they have to grow flowers, they are to be cultivated and mixed with the soil being pulverized (V-3). Before planting, a proper worship to Brahmana, Siva and the tree is to be performed by the owner of the garden (V-4). Vrksayurveda Text found in Matsya-purana: All the eighteen Puranas contain ancient historical traditions of Aryans. The process of editing these Puranas had already started in the Vedic period, as mentioned in the vratya sukta of the Atharva-veda , Chandogya Upanisd and other Sanskrit texts. Puranas explain the teachings of the Vedas and meant for all ma types of people. In the hymns of Vedas,we see a few glimpses of the most ancient states, but the authors of the Puranas try to give the lists of these states and their relation with each other. The Purans help us to construct the map of Vedic India. The Vedas mentioned the name of several deities and seers associated with the different Aryan Cult. The Purans provide us with details about these cults and their evolution, social customs and laws.The Puranas were meant for the masses. The Sutas who

126 recited Puranas were very much popular among the masses. They used to propagate knowledge contained in the Puranas in a very interesting manner. Thus the tradition of the society relating to the plants and herbs are found in the Puranas in a more detail form. The three oldest Puranas are the Matsya purana (Matsyapurana), the Brahamanda and Vayu-purana. Certain references are found in this Matsyapurana about the plants and their uses in the ancient days of human civilization by the kings and the citizens. The Matsyapurana in its chapter 255 deals with the Vastu-Vidya for constructing houses. After construction it directs to plant trees near the house for the welfare of the owner. In verse (20-24) the Purana mentions that, 'A banyan tree is to be planted to the east of the house as it is auspicious and it also fulfills all desires. It also suggests a fig tree to the south, a peepal tree on the west, and plaksa tree to the north of the house as it brings fortune to the owner. If ketaki trees, milky trees, asana trees and the straight trees is planted in the house in the above mentioned directions respectively then it means misery to the lady of the house and her children.
