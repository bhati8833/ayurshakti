Plants have medicinal value. In Ayurveda "The science of life" is a product of plants and herbs. The Sanskrit word for medicine is 'osadhi'. The first part of this word 'osa' means malady and pollution. So, that which destroys and removes the ill 84 Sadhale Nalini, Tr., Vishvavallabha, P. 76

173 effects of maladies and pollutions is called 'Oshadi' (medicine). The herbal plants are grown out of the wrath of God Rudra and hence naturally capable of destroying the maladies and pollution. These plants and vegetation delights the work, hence these are called 'delight' (mudah). The medicinal plants delight the people since they remove several ills and diseases of human beings. Thus plants and vegetation are sources of human well-being and (Satapatha Brahman-2 .2.4.5; Kausha Brahman 6.5; Shatapatha Brahman-9. 4. 1. 7)85 happiness. Plants protect us from natural calamities like flood, famine and soil erosion. Plants are essential for sacred occasions, and are considered auspicious. Caraka Samhita considers the destruction of forests as the most dangerous act of humanity. The destruction of forests is most dangerous for the nation and for human beings. Vegetation has direct tie with well-being of society. Due to the pollution of natural environment and destruction of vanaspati, many diseases crop up to ruin the nation.
