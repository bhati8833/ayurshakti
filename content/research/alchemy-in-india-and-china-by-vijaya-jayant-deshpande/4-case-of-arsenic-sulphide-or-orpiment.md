4. Case of Arsenic Sulphide or Orpiment

Sanskrit terms for various metals and chemical 3 salts were known to the Chinese. Furthermore, the Sanskrit names were sometimes given as synonyms to the Chinese ones. An alchemical text of Tang dynasty (618-906 AD) gives a synonym for arsenic sulphide. 3 See Mahavyutpatti in Primary Sources. This dictionary gives Chinese names for Sindura, Hingula, Visoda, Tanganaksara, tutthaka, etc.
