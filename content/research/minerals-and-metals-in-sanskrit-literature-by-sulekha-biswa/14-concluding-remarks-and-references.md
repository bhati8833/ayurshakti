It is thus established that Kautiliya Arthasastra records Indians' skill in gem minerals, metallic ores, metals, alloys and the end-products as well as an aptitude for scientific methodology during the subcontinent's early Historical Period. The references to the South Indian gems and red corals from the West have induced some scholars to suggest that the Arthasastra

Most scholars have missed a cardinal point about the Arthesastre. Although the text deals with technology, economics and politics, and accepts the Vedic relgion, it does not subscribe to the views (1.2.2-7) that any one of the above subjects should occupy supreme position in human society. Kautilya gave maximum importance to Anviksiki, which may be translated as philosophy of critical investigation based upon reasoning, and which constituted of Sankhya, Yoga and Lokavata (all these were non-Vedic) philosophical, systems. Anviksiki was supposed to bring out the merits as well as the fallacies of economics, politics and also the Vedic religion: "Investigating, by means of reasoning. (hetubhih anviksamana ), what is spiritual good and evil in the three Vedas (fit dharmadharmo trayyam  ), gain and loss in economics (arthanartho varttayam  ). good policy and bad policy in the science of politics, as well as the relative strength and weakness (() of these three sciences, philosophy confers benefit on the people, keeps the mind steady in adversity and in prosperity and brings about proficiency

V-29 in thought, speech and action" (1.2.11).
