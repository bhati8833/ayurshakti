The varieties of zircon can be distinguished from other gems through their remarkably high specific gravity (4.61-4.82) and characteristic hardness 7 which is slightly more than that of garnet (hardness 7 and sp.gr. 3.6-3.7) and much less than that of diamond (hardness 10 and sp.gr. 3.52). The name zircon came either from jargon (the worthless diamond') or Arabic Zargun (Zare gold and gun colour in Persian). The Indian Ratnasastra texts referred to light yellow jargon as well as reddish-yellow hyacinth variety of Zircon.
