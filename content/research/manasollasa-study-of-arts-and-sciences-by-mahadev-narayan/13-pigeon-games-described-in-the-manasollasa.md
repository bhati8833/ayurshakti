13. Pigeon games described in the Manasollasa

Somesvara tells Paravata-Vinoda or amusement through pigeons. He describes four types of Pigeons on the basis of the four Varnas of men, namely : ( 1 ) Brahmana, (2) Ksatriya, (3) vaisya and (4) Sudra. The Pigeons of Brahmana, Ksatriya, and Valsya types are born particularly in Sindhu-desa, while the Pigeons of Sudra jati are seldom available. 110 109. kunjaraviva durdharso mahisau yodhyennrpah | praharantau visanagraih kopanmilitamastakau ||  4.10.1272. 110. paravatavinodasya varnanam kriyate'dhuna | sindhudese visesena brahmanah catriya visah || paravatah prajayante sudrah syuryatra kutracit | sasikha padapicchadya vicarakvananasa linah || jneyaste brahmanajatya pavitrah subhadarsanah | padapiccheh samayuktah sasvatkvananakovidah ||  4.11.1277-1279.

139 Further Somesvara gives the characteristics of each Varna. The Pigeons of Brahmana species are pure and beautiful. They have got a tuft of hair on their heads. The Ksatriya type does not have the tuft of hair and is full of Padapicehas. The pigeons of Vaisya jati also do not have a tuft of hair but they are of different colours, like white, black, red, yellow, green, black and red-spotted 112 etc. The sudra types of Pigeons have green limbs, blue neck, black spotted wings and red eyes and legs. They are the adhama or the lowest types of pigeons. Besides these four types, there are Pigeons of Candala or Antyaja jati.
