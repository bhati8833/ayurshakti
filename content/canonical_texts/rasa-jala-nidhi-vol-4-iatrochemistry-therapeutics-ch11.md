## 152. Treatment for fever (114): Jvara-nrisimha rasa

Rasa Jala Nidhi, vol 4: Iatrochemistry

This fourth volume of the Rasa-jala-nidhi deals with Rasa-chikitsa-vidya, also known a the science of Iatrchemistry (chemical medicine), a major branch of Ayurveda. It contains Ayurvedic treatments for Fever and Diarrhea.

Treatment for fever (114): Jvara-nrisimha rasa

Equal quantities of mercury, sulphur, orpiment, and bhallataka are to be rubbed together with the milk of snuhi, and subjected to heat by puta, for six hours. When cooled of itself, the medicine is to be taken out and subjected to bhavana for three days with the juices of bhringaraja leaves, ganda-durba, and roots of chitraka, and then powdered. This medicine cures chaturthaka and other chronic fevers. Diet, soup of mudga grams, and milk, to be given after remission of fever.

Conclusion:

This concludes ‘Treatment for fever (114): Jvara-nrisimha rasa’ included in Bhudeb Mookerjee Rasa Jala Nidhi, vol 4: Initiation, Mercury and Laboratory. ## 166. Treatment for fever (129): Rasaraja rasa

Rasa Jala Nidhi, vol 4: Iatrochemistry

This fourth volume of the Rasa-jala-nidhi deals with Rasa-chikitsa-vidya, also known a the science of Iatrchemistry (chemical medicine), a major branch of Ayurveda. It contains Ayurvedic treatments for Fever and Diarrhea.

Treatment for fever (129): Rasaraja rasa

One part of mercury, one part of copper pyrites, two parts of realgar, three parts of sulphur, ten parts of orpiment, five parts of copper, and three parts of bhallataka are to be rubbed together and saturated with the milk of snuhi. The compound is then to be kept inside a strong earthen pot, the mouth of which is to be closed by means of an earthen basin, the joint being cemented with mud and rags. The pot is then to be heated for twelve hours. When cooled by radiation of heat, the medicine is to be taken out and powdered very fine. Dose, four raktis each, to be taken with honey and juice of betel leaves. This medicine cures all sorts of fevers.

Conclusion:

This concludes ‘Treatment for fever (129): Rasaraja rasa’ included in Bhudeb Mookerjee Rasa Jala Nidhi, vol 4: Initiation, Mercury and Laboratory.
