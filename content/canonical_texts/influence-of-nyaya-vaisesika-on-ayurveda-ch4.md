3. Origin and development of Nyaya-Sastra

Origin and development of Nyaya-Sastra The Brahmanas maintain that their religion is eternal It is based on scriptures which are said to eternal; but revealed in different periods of time of seers or sages called Rsis. These scriptures are called Vedas, which comprise the Samhitas and Brahmanas. The Brahmanas called the doctrine of soul and its destiny profounded in the Aranyakas has existed in Indian from the beginning of time. They tried to place the Brahmanic religion one of firm basic unshaken by the influence of time. It does not however find favour with modern scholars. Their view was all human civilization, including the Indian people grew up by a process of evolution. So the conception of soul like evrything else has under-gone stages of development in the course of ages, these stages may clearly be seen in the doctrine of the souls as given in the samhitas, Brahmanas and Upanisads. 6
