### Book 4 - Cikitsā-sthāna (Therapeutics & Disease Management)
1. **Jvara Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Management of acute, chronic, and malarial fevers.
2. **Raktapitta Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Treatments for bleeding disorders.
3. **Kāsa Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Herbal remedies for cough and bronchitis.
4. **Śvāsa & Hidhmā Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Asthma protocols and bronchial relaxation.
5. **Rājayakṣmā Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Restorative therapies for chronic wasting diseases.
6. **Chardi, Hṛdroga & Tṛṣṇā Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Remedies for vomiting, cardiac ailments, and morbid thirst.
7. **Madātyaya Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Detoxification from alcohol and narcotic toxicity.
8. **Arṣas Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Medical and surgical management of hemorrhoids.
9. **Atīsāra Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Antidiarrheal and astringent remedies.
10. **Grahani Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Restoration of digestive fire (Agni) and malabsorption cure.
11. **Mūtrāghāta Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Diuretic formulations and stone-dissolving herbs.
12. **Prameha Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Management of diabetes mellitus and hyperglycemia.
13. **Vidradhi & Vṛddhi Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Treatments for deep abscesses and scrotal swellings.
14. **Gulma Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Herbal and purgative remedies for abdominal masses.
15. **Udara Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Treatment of ascites and fluid retention.
16. **Pāṇḍu Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Hematinic and liver-tonic formulations for anemia.
17. **Śvayathu Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Management of generalized and localized edema.
18. **Visarpa Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Cooling and blood-purifying therapies for erysipelas.
19. **Kuṣṭha Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Purificatory and topical remedies for skin diseases.
20. **Śvitra & Kṛmi Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Vitiligo remedies and anthelmintic formulations.
21. **Vātavyādhi Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Oil therapies, enemas, and herbs for neurological disease.
22. **Vātarakta Cikitsita Adhyāya** — Bloodletting and anti-gout herbal formulations.
