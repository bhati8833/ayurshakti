11. **Sandhisitāsita Roga Pratiṣedha Adhyāya** — Therapeutics for conjunctival disorders.
12. **Dṛṣṭiroga Vijñānīya Adhyāya** — Cataracts, vision impairment, and retinal diseases.

15. **Sarvākṣiroga Vijñānīya Adhyāya** — Generalized ocular inflammation and infections.
16. **Sarvākṣiroga Pratiṣedha Adhyāya** — Treatments for pan-ophthalmic diseases.
17. **Karṇaroga Vijñānīya Adhyāya** — Ear ache, otitis, and hearing impairment diagnosis.
18. **Karṇaroga Pratiṣedha Adhyāya** — Ear drops and ear treatments.
19. **Nāsāroga Vijñānīya Adhyāya** — Rhinitis, sinusitis, and nasal polyps.
20. **Nāsāroga Pratiṣedha Adhyāya** — Treatment of nasal ailments.
21. **Mukharoga Vijñānīya Adhyāya** — Diseases of the mouth, gums, teeth, and tongue.
22. **Mukharoga Pratiṣedha Adhyāya** — Oral treatments and gargling remedies.
23. **Śiroroga Vijñānīya Adhyāya** — Headaches, migraines, and cranial pathology.
24. **Śiroroga Pratiṣedha Adhyāya** — Nasya and head therapies for headache.
25. **Vraṇa Pratiṣedha Adhyāya** — Ulcer care, wound healing, and antiseptic dressings.
26. **Sadyovraṇa Pratiṣedha Adhyāya** — Traumatic wounds, hemorrhages, and emergency care.
27. **Bhaṅga Pratiṣedha Adhyāya** — Fractures, dislocations, and bone setters' techniques.
28. **Bhagandara Pratiṣedha Adhyāya** — Anal fistula surgical and Ksharasutra cure.
29. **Granthyarbuda & Ślīpada Vijñānīya Adhyāya** — Tumors, cysts, and filariasis pathology.
30. **Granthyarbuda & Ślīpada Pratiṣedha Adhyāya** — Treatment of cysts and elephantiasis.
31. **Kṣudraroga Vijñānīya Adhyāya** — Minor skin conditions, warts, moles, and acne.
32. **Kṣudraroga Pratiṣedha Adhyāya** — Topical pastes for minor skin conditions.
33. **Guhyaroga Vijñānīya Adhyāya** — Reproductive organ ailments and venereal afflictions.
34. **Guhyaroga Pratiṣedha Adhyāya** — Gynecological and urological remedies.
35. **Viṣapratiṣedha Adhyāya** — General toxicology and poison neutralizing antidotes.
36. **Sarpaviṣapratiṣedha Adhyāya** — Snakebite antivenoms and emergency management.
37. **Kīṭalūtādi Viṣapratiṣedha Adhyāya** — Insect bites, spider toxicity, and scorpion stings.
38. **Rasāyana Adhyāya** — Anti-aging, immune-boosting rejuvenative tonics.
39. **Vājīkaraṇa Adhyāya** — Reproductive vigor, aphrodisiacs, and semen quality.
40. **Vājīkaraṇapradīpa Adhyāya** — Advanced formulas for vitality and lineage preservation.
