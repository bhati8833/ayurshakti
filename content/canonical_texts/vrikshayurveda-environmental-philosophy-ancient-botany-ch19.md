7. Trees in association with Constellations

IV.6. Trees in association with Constellations:—It is a scientifically proved phenomenon that the mysterious celestial bodies do have a constant influence on the earth's biosphere. A 0 205 discipline of astronomy says that the existence of twenty-seven constellations or naksatras has tremendous impact over the entire living world. However, Indian astrology takes three elements into consideration the celestial bodies, the nature and the human world. It is further said that some celestial gem stones, metals in association with specific trees and plants conditioned the intensity of the adverse or favourable impacts of star and planets on mankind. Hence, the rearing and protection of the favourable plants and trees which transmits as agents of the respective star power is thus reflected herewith along with their valuable properties.
