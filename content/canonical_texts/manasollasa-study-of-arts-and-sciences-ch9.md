6. Entertainments reflected in Somesvara’s writings

The social life, particularly the urban culture, reflected in Somesvara's writings is essentially characterised by an atmosphere of luxury and gaiety with an unmistakable element of sophistication. Both the urban and the rural people had their own means of amusement which included various socio-cultural festivals referred to above. These festivals provided much genuine amusement 1 in the form of amateur exercises in various arts and literary works.
