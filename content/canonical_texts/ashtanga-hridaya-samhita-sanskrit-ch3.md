## 📜 Historical & Scholarly Context

In the lineage of ancient Indian medicine, **Acharya Vagbhata** occupies a unique position. While Acharya Charaka focused deeply on internal medicine, etiology, and consciousness, and Acharya Sushruta pioneered surgical interventions and human anatomy, Vagbhata recognized the need for a practical, lucid, and unified handbook for medical practitioners.

The title **Aṣṭāṅga Hṛdaya** literally translates to *"The Heart or Core Essence of the Eight Branches of Ayurveda"*. The text compresses the sprawling wisdom of earlier classical works into approximately 7,120 poetic verses. It is praised across traditional Indian universities for its clarity, logical organization, and therapeutic precision.
