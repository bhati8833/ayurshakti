### Book 2 - Śārīra-sthāna (Anatomy, Embryology & Vital Points)
1. **Garbhāvakrānti Adhyāya** — Conception, fetal development, and maternal care.
2. **Garbhavyāpad Adhyāya** — Complications of pregnancy and fetal management.
3. **Aṅgavibhāga Śārīra Adhyāya** — Anatomical structures, body systems, and organ classification.
4. **Marmavibhāga Śārīra Adhyāya** — Detailed study of 107 vital anatomical energy points (Marmas).
5. **Prakṛti Vijñānīya Adhyāya** — Constitutional analysis (Vata, Pitta, Kapha Prakriti types).
6. **Vikṛti Vijñānīya Adhyāya** — Prognostic signs, fatal symptoms (Arishta Lakshanas), and mortality indicators.
