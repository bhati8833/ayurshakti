These plants and vegetation delights the work, hence these are called 'delight' (mudah). The medicinal plants delight the people since they remove several ills and diseases of human beings. Thus plants and vegetation are sources of human well-being and (Satapatha Brahman-2 .2.4.5; Kausha Brahman 6.5; Shatapatha Brahman-9. 4. 1. 7)85 happiness.
