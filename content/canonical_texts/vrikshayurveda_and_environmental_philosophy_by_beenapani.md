0. 1.8. Tradition of Vrksayurveda texts in Sanskrit literature: The term Vrikshayurveda which means plant science or the science of plants is to be divided as vrksayuh and Veda. It is the Veda which refers science of plant life or longevity of plants (vrksayuh). Vrikshayurveda is thus the science of rearing of plants. In other words it is the science that resulted from the application of ayurveda to the phenomena of plants. Though the word Vrikshayurveda as a science of plant rearing became a systematic branch of study in later Sanskrit litreture still the enumeration about this topic is to be found from very ancient Vedic texts such as Veda, Purana, Upanisads, Aranyaka, Brahmanas, Samhitas, Vyakarana, etc. Vyakarana, etc. But the systematic representation of the topic is to be found in some specific texts. 7. In Sanskrit literature the word vrksa gives the impression of a punlinga sabda the outcome of which is in the form of brasca + ksa. The author here has named the book as "Visvavallabha" [Viswa-Vallabha] because the subject of plant life is dearer to all. Environmental Philosophy behind the determination of Auspicious & Inauspicious directions for planting of Trees near the Abode:—In an environment, full of danger to life, the trees indeed are the friends to human life and animal life. There is a possibility of a son going way ward, become harmful and dangerous in the society, but there is no such possibility of such hazards in the case of a tree. So, everyone bows down to tree, the supreme preceptor, who preaches to the world the lesson of self-sacrifice for the sake of serving others.
