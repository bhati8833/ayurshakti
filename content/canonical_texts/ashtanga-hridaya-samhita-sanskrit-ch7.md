> **Clinical Executive Summary (E-E-A-T Overview)**: Detailed classical discussion on # Book 1 - Sūtra-sthāna (Fundamental Principles & Hygiene) within Ashtanga Hridaya Samhita Sanskrit. Formatted with classical Sanskrit attributions and modern international clinical commentary by Suresh Bhati.

### Book 1 - Sūtra-sthāna (Fundamental Principles & Hygiene)
1. **Āyuṣkāmīya Adhyāya** — Longevity, goals of life, and fundamental Ayurvedic principles.
2. **Dinacaryā Adhyāya** — Daily routine, oral hygiene, oil pulling, exercise, and sleep.
3. **Ṛtucaryā Adhyāya** — Seasonal habits, dietary adjustments for 6 Indian seasons.
4. **Rogānutpādanīya Adhyāya** — Non-suppression of 13 natural bodily urges.
5. **Dravadravyavijñānīya Adhyāya** — Qualities of water, milk, sugarcane juice, honey, and oils.
6. **Annasvarūpavijñānīya Adhyāya** — Food grains, legumes, meats, vegetables, and cooking methods.
7. **Annarakṣā Adhyāya** — Protection of food from contamination, poisons, and unwholesome combinations.
8. **Mātrāśitīya Adhyāya** — Ideal food quantity, digestion timing, and post-prandial drinks.
9. **Dravyādhivijñānīya Adhyāya** — Pharmacological concepts (Rasa, Guna, Virya, Vipaka, Prabhava).
10. **Rasabhedīya Adhyāya** — Six tastes (Sweet, Sour, Salty, Pungent, Bitter, Astringent) and dosha actions.
11. **Doṣādivijñānīya Adhyāya** — Functions and pathology of Vata, Pitta, Kapha, Dhatus, and Malas.
12. **Doṣabhedīya Adhyāya** — Sub-types of Vata (Prana, Udana, Vyana, Samana, Apana), Pitta, and Kapha.
13. **Doṣopakramaṇīya Adhyāya** — General principles of doshic treatment and balance.
14. **Dvividhopakramaṇīya Adhyāya** — Nourishing (*Brimhana*) versus Depleting (*Langhana*) therapies.
15. **Śodhanādigaṇasaṅgraha Adhyāya** — Grouping of medicinal herbs for purificatory procedures.
16. **Snehavidhi Adhyāya** — Snehana (Oleation therapy) methods and indicators.
17. **Svedavidhi Adhyāya** — Svedana (Sudation/Fomentation therapy) types and safety rules.
18. **Vamanavirecanavidhi Adhyāya** — Emesis (Vamana) and Purgation (Virechana) protocols.
19. **Bastividhi Adhyāya** — Medicated enema (Niruha & Anuvasana Basti) procedures.
20. **Nasyavidhi Adhyāya** — Nasal administration of medicated oils and powders.
21. **Dhūmapānavidhi Adhyāya** — Inhalation of medicated herbal smoke.
22. **Gaṇḍūṣādividhi Adhyāya** — Oil pulling (Gandusha) and mouth gargling (Kavala).
23. **Āścātanāñjanavidhi Adhyāya** — Ocular instillation and eye collyrium.
24. **Tarpaṇapuṭapākavidhi Adhyāya** — Medicated eye baths (Netra Tarpana).
25. **Yantravidhi Adhyāya** — Blunt surgical instruments and applicators.
26. **Śastravidhi Adhyāya** — Sharp surgical instruments and cutting techniques.
27. **Sirāvyadhavidhi Adhyāya** — Venesection and bloodletting protocols.
28. **Śalyāharaṇavidhi Adhyāya** — Surgical extraction of foreign objects.
29. **Śastrakarmavidhi Adhyāya** — Operative procedures, incisions, and suturing.
30. **Kṣārāgnikarmavidhi Adhyāya** — Alkaline cautery (Kshara Karma) and Thermal cautery (Agni Karma).
