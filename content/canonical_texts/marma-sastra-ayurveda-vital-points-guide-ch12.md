Study of Vasti Marma

Etymology of Vasti (Vasthi, Basti, Basthi)—"Vas = The term Vasthi is derived from the root Nivasey Nabheradah. The meaning of which is the place where the urine is collected. Description The situation of the marma is within the pelvis corresponding to bladder, the wall is fibroelastic with a few plain muscle fibres and blood vessels. 159

Injury to this marma, as a whole endangers life immediately. This may be due to the complications or to shock by stimulation of the vesical, the hypogastric nerve plexus and the nerves in the wall of the bladder. This is a sadya pranahara marma and is four anguli in extent. Vaghbata describes the shape of Vasti (bladder) to be Danurvakram (curved like a bow) and having one outlet in the lower part. Its bhuta structure is Agni. Adhidevatha is Rudra. 160

According to Charaka, Vasti, which is the receptacle of urine, is situated amidst Stoola Guda (large intestines and rectum), Mushka (testes), Sevanee (phrenum) and the ducts that carry semen and urine. The bladder is to all the water bearing ducts, as is the sea to all the stream. Injury to this marma may cause obstruction to vata, mutra and mala, pain in the groin, penis and bladder. Symptoms like running nose, fainting, loss of bladder control are seen. Kulayana marma Jalapandam The area of this marma is three angula above the navel and four angula below the Thila kuzhi marma.
