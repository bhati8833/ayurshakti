### Book 3 - Nidāna-sthāna (Pathology & Disease Etiology)
1. **Sarvaroga Nidāna Adhyāya** — General principles of disease causation and pathogenesis (Samprapti).
2. **Jvara Nidāna Adhyāya** — Classification and diagnosis of fevers.
3. **Raktapitta & Kāsa Nidāna Adhyāya** — Hemorrhagic disorders and cough classifications.
4. **Śvāsa & Hidhmā Nidāna Adhyāya** — Respiratory distress, asthma, and hiccups.
5. **Rājayakṣmā Nidāna Adhyāya** — Tuberculosis and debilitating wasting diseases.
6. **Madātyaya Nidāna Adhyāya** — Alcohol toxicity, intoxication, and withdrawal.
7. **Arṣas Nidāna Adhyāya** — Etiology and types of hemorrhoids (piles).
8. **Atīsāra & Grahani Nidāna Adhyāya** — Diarrhea, dysentery, and irritable bowel disorders.
9. **Mūtrāghāta Nidāna Adhyāya** — Urinary retention, renal calculi, and dysuria.
10. **Prameha Nidāna Adhyāya** — Metabolic syndrome, diabetes mellitus, and urinary anomalies.
11. **Vidradhi & Gulma Nidāna Adhyāya** — Abscesses, abdominal tumors, and cystic growths.
12. **Udara Nidāna Adhyāya** — Ascites, liver enlargement, and abdominal enlargement.
13. **Pāṇḍu, Śopha & Visarpa Nidāna Adhyāya** — Anemia, edema, and erysipelas/skin eruptions.
14. **Kuṣṭha & Kṛmi Nidāna Adhyāya** — Chronic skin disorders (leprosy/psoriasis) and parasitic infestations.
15. **Vātavyādhi Nidāna Adhyāya** — Neurological disorders, paralysis, sciatica, and neuromuscular afflictions.
16. **Vātarakta Nidāna Adhyāya** — Gouty arthritis and vascular inflammatory disorders.
