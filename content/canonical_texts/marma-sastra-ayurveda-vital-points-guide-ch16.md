Study of Kukundara Marma

Kukundara Etymology—The word Kukundara is derived from the word Kundara which is prefixed by the word Ku. Ku is usually used to denote nindyartha as in Kuprasidhi, Kutsitha, down, notorious. Kukundara means Omal Kuzhi or a small depression near the buttocks. Description Situation of these marmas is on both sides of vertebral column, corresponding to the sacroiliac articulation anterior to which the Sciatic nerve passes. Injury to this marma leads to complete anesthesia and inability to use the lower extremity. They are two in number and are sandhi marmas, at the same time they are vaikalyakara also. They are half anguli in extent. Its bhuta structure is Soumya. Adhidevatha is Raudra. Surface Anatomy The Kukundara marma falls within the pelvic cavity. The palpation of the marma can be started as stated for the katikatharuna simultaneously with both hands/ by tracing the margin of sacral bone on both sides. It is possible to feel the tenderness on the line. 195

Transversus abdominismuscle and aponeurosis Multifidus muscle. This is included in prishta marma. Prishta means back. Arunadatta in his commentary states that Kukundaro nimnakara. It has the shape of the dimple. Another statement of Susruta Parswayor Jaghana bahir bhaga Dalhana comments. Parswa as vama and Daksina bhaga. 196

Hence it could be inferred Kukundara as two dimples on either side of the spines located in the loin. Among the 14 prishta marmas, Kukundara marma is the only marma that comes under the sandhi classification. On deep dissection one may find this area Sacro iliac articulation. Symptoms of injury narrated in various Ayurvedic texts are so vivid and complete, that nothing need to be added or deleted from the text even now. Sparshajnana paraplegia. □ Chesta Hani = loss of motor activity o Sensory loss. Both clubbed together lead to complete Tamil varma sastra According to the varma sastra four fingers below the ullellu varma lies Pinnellu varma. The symptoms are whole body swelling, irritant and prolonged cough, sore throat. Accumulation of phlegm in the chest, fever with vomiting. Start treatment within 7 nazhigai. Arasa varma is situated little below the pinnellu adjacent to the spinal cord. Injury causes fever, irritable bowel, weakness and numbness over the extremities. 197

Start remedial measures immediately. Internal medicines should be started only after 51 Nazhikai.

Study of Amsaphalaka Marma

Amsaphalaka Etymology—This is the combination of two word Amsa and Phalaka. This literally means flat surface near the shoulder. Description Situation of these marmas is on both sides of vertebral column and on the upper most part of the back. These are two in number and are Asthi marmas and vaikalyakaram also. It is of half anguli in 206

extent. Its bhuta structure is Jala. Adhidevatha is Iswara. Injury to this marma leads to numbness or atrophy of upper limbs due to injury of the nerves on the anterior surface of the scapula. Surface Anatomy Thus there are two Amsapalaka marma located on the medial edges of the scapular blade. Injury to the nerve may cause palsy, as in Brachial palsy and axillary nerve palsy. Hence the sages have rightly classified this as vaikalyakara group Tamil varma sastra According to the Varma Suttiram there are seven varma seen in and around this area They are 1. Arali, 2. Menmai 3. Vinodam, 4. Alli, 5. Ullellu, 6. Punool, 7. Thappiran. Arali is also called Arani. This is situated one span away from the pidari varma on the back side along the vertebra. This should be treated within 27 nazhigai. Symptoms like abdominal swelling lack of interest and abdominal pains are seen. 208

Menmai varma is seen four fingers below the arali varmam. Polyuria,diffuse whole body swelling, palpitation and chest pain are seen. Treatment should be started within 6 nazhigai. Any injury to Vinota varmam may cause death within 7 1/2 nazhigai. He may be medically treated immediately. Alli Varmam is above the Vinota varmam at a distance of five anguli lies the alli varmam. Injury leads to excessive chillness, abdomen becomes flatulent. Start treatment within 7 nazhigai or death occurs within 57 nazhigai. One span away the alli varmam is ullellu varmam. Injury to this cause extensive damage to the skull (Ullellu means skull). Temperature drops coughs and dyspnoeae develops. Start treatment within 6 nazhigai. Punul varmam is close to munnellu varma and Ani kalam. It is three fingers below the Ani kalam. Temperature rises and victims feel extremely tired. Injury to Tappira varmam renders limb immobile. The body feel hot with cold extremities. Patient develops delirium. Treatment should be started within seven nazhigai. 209

Study of Brihati Marma

Brihati Etymology—Brihati is developed from Brih vridhau (Augment). Other meanings are . Upper garments. A mantle A wrapper. Description This marma is situated on the posterior side corresponding to the base of breast lateral to the vertebral column on the both sides. These are two in number and are sira marmas and kalanthara pranahara also. It is of half anguli in extent. Its bhuta structure is Agni and Jala. Adhidevatha is Iswara. Injury to these marmas leads to continuous bleeding and endangers life in course of the time due to complications. Surface Anatomy This marma apparently falls near the kidney located inside. Beneath this point are arterial branches of the kidney are located. However these parts are not palpable superficially on the back. It is easier to probe these marma with the thumbs simultaneously on the sides of thoracic vertebrae (8th- 9th). Anatomical Structures corresponding to this marma ☐ On the right side the vessels at hilum of liver. 204

Injury to this area will cause heavy bleeding and thereby death. 205

Tamil varma sastra Azhal varma is five fingers below the arasa varma close to the spinal column. Injury leads to delirium. Treatment should be started within seven nazhigai. Ull varmam is situated four fingers below the azhal varmam. Symptoms such as delirium. Incontinence of urine are seen when afflicted. Kulayana marma The marma Tharippan is located 6 angulas below the nipple on the back. If injured the patient faints and later on develop dull nagging ache over the body.
