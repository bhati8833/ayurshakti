246 Epilogue The different chapter reviews made in this work describing the different aspects of Vrksayurveda put forth a thematic view of the subject concerned in the form of a research work. The above chapter study gives a vivid impression that the ancient Indian thinkers do had a conscious, thorough and definite idea about the interrelationship between human and the plants. In fact, the inception of the awareness about preservation of the natural resources was well realized by the traditional Indians. The aspects like the knowledge on biogenetic resources, bio-spherical classification of different plants and trees, association of plants with conctellations and planets having impact upon the favourable and unfavourable conditions of the living beings, the environmental values, significance, religious importance, ethical code of conduct, aesthetical outlines and appreciations, philosophical reviews, etc. are being discussed with exhaustive study references. However, the study reflects that the ancient human beings were consciously aware of the role of nature in the growth and development of the human life. Thus the famous vedic prayer goes on as -

247 om dyauh santih/ antariksam santih /prthivi santih/ apah santih/ ausadhaya santih /vanaspatayah santih/ visvedevah santih/ brahma santih/sarvam santih/santireva santih /sa ma santiredhi/ om santih santih santih // (Sukla Yajur Veda, 36.17) The connotation prescribed in the well known Vedic mantra for peace in every sphere of the cosmic world above implies for the aspects of human welfare, peaceful living, being made possible only when the surrounding environment both including the earth as well as sky and arial region maintain a proper balance. It is thus rightly coined by the grammarians (Jayaditya and Vamana) of >> 184 Kasika Vrtti as "Sarvasmai hitam" or which refers for a desire of wellbeing of everyone. Further, in exemplifying such wisdom it may be interpreted in the context that it is the duty of every human being that they should protect both the movable and immovable components of the living environment taking consideration onto their usefulness giving importance to human health and welfare aspects. However, the modern ecologists and environmentalists come-up with an assertion that for human beings they should consider the abrupt needs and usefulness of both the living and non-living objects for them and should always be prepared with the possible ways to preserve them to lead a happy and peaceful life. Moreover, it specifically means and refers to the control of the 184. Kasika Vrtti, 5.1.1.

248 wanton destruction of the nature for fulfillment of their selfish desires. In reference to the idiom, "sarvasmai hitam" connotation has also been expressed in Patanjali Mahabhasyam and Manusmrti referring to the goodness towards all. Thus, in support of such expression Manusmrti even has entered a dictum as follows; so' abhidhyaya sarirat svat sisrksur vividhah prajah | apa eva sasarjadau tasu bijamavasrjat || (Manusmrti, 1.8) The extract of the above stated dictum refers to the desirous action enacted by the almighty creator who being desirous to produce beings of many kinds from his own body, at first created the basic component called water and placed His sheer vintage by means of a seed within it. In fact, before creating His beautiful creation God has taken conscious attempts to maintain its balance and created every object (both living and non-living) purposefully with a motive behind the whole creation. However, His basic purpose was to maintain a perfect balance of the ecology. In Bhagvat Gita, words of lord Krisna also depict the same saying. chinnadvaidha yatatmanah sarvabhuta hite ratah || (Bhagvat Gita, karmasanyasa yoga, v.25)

249 The saying however speaks for "one who is desirous for the wellbeing of all other creatures in turn attains moksa". Especially the protection and care towards the plants will protect the world and the Vrksayurveda texts have instructed this through practical teachings on Plant rearing and also through religious and philosophical instructions for the betterment of the living society.

243 VI.8. Environmental Philosophy behind the Growth of Trees as influenced by the Implantation of certain other types of Trees: The rationality behind the growth of trees influencing the favourable yield of other crops is in other way called as plant indicators for crops. Therefore, the author indicates the presence of nyagrodha to be fir for yielding barley crop, tinduka being good for svastika and asvattha tree being favorable for all type of crops (v.320). Likewise, jambu tree is favorable for the growth of sesame and black gram, sirisa tree fit for green gram, madhuka being favorable for the production of wheat and saptaparna fit for yielding barley crop (v.321). These prescriptions prove the indigenous knowledge of farming.
