> **Clinical Executive Summary (Volume Overview)**: Comprehensive classical Ayurvedic guide to toxicology (Kalpasthana), emergency snakebite antidotes, plant & mineral toxins, rabies management, and sonic detoxification. Formatted for modern global E-E-A-T research reference.

## Volume Chapter Directory

### 1. [Ayurvedic Food Safety and Poison Detection: Sushruta Samhita Kalpasthana Chapter 1](/articles/ayurvedic-food-safety-and-poison-detection-sushruta-samhita-kalpasthana-ch1)
**Overview**: Royal food protection, detection of poisoned drinks, food safety protocols, and immediate resuscitation methods.

### 2. [Plant and Mineral Toxicology Guide: Sushruta Samhita Kalpasthana Chapter 2](/articles/plant-and-mineral-toxicology-guide-sushruta-samhita-kalpasthana-ch2)
**Overview**: Classification of immobile (Sthavara) vegetable and mineral poisons, botanical toxins, and systemic antidote formulations.

### 3. [Animal Venom Classification and Air Purification: Sushruta Samhita Kalpasthana Chapter 3](/articles/animal-venom-classification-and-air-purification-sushruta-samhita-kalpasthana-ch3)
**Overview**: Classification of animal (Jangama) toxins, venomous bites, and environmental air/water decontamination techniques.

### 4. [Venomous Snakes and Bite Symptoms: Sushruta Samhita Kalpasthana Chapter 4](/articles/venomous-snakes-and-bite-symptoms-sushruta-samhita-kalpasthana-ch4)
**Overview**: Taxonomy of venomous serpents (Hooded, Spotted, Striped), physiological bite stages, and clinical prognosis.

### 5. [Emergency Snakebite Antidotes and Protocols: Sushruta Samhita Kalpasthana Chapter 5](/articles/emergency-snakebite-antidotes-and-protocols-sushruta-samhita-kalpasthana-ch5)
**Overview**: Classical anti-venomous Agadas, emergency tourniquet & suction protocols, and resuscitation formulations (Ajeya Ghrita).

### 6. [Ayurvedic Management of Rabies and Hydrophobia: Sushruta Samhita Kalpasthana Chapter 6](/articles/ayurvedic-management-of-rabies-and-hydrophobia-sushruta-samhita-kalpasthana-ch6)
**Overview**: Rodent venom, rabid animal bites (Alarka Visha), clinical symptoms of hydrophobia, and cauterization therapies.

### 7. [Sonic Detoxification Therapy and Master Antidotes: Sushruta Samhita Kalpasthana Chapter 7](/articles/sonic-detoxification-therapy-and-master-antidotes-sushruta-samhita-kalpasthana-ch7)
**Overview**: Therapeutic anti-venomous sound drums (Dundubhi Svaniya), Kshara-agada, Kalyanaka Ghrita, and Mahasugandhi Agada.

### 8. [Medical Management of Insect and Spider Venom: Sushruta Samhita Kalpasthana Chapter 8](/articles/medical-management-of-insect-and-spider-venom-sushruta-samhita-kalpasthana-ch8)
**Overview**: Insects, scorpions, and venomous spider (Luta) bites, staging of necrotic tissue lesions, and specific antidote pastes.
