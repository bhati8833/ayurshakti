# Natural Remedies for Dog Itchy Skin: An Ayurvedic Approach

> **Clinical Executive Summary**: This guide on **Natural Remedies for Dog Itchy Skin: An Ayurvedic Approach** synthesizes classical Sanskrit Samhita principles with modern botanical pharmacological research. Curated under the editorial supervision of Suresh Bhati, it provides actionable doshic guidelines, evidence-based applications, and safety parameters.

Chronic scratching, paw licking, red inflammation, and seasonal allergies in dogs are classic manifestations of an elevated **Pitta and Kapha imbalance** in the skin tissue layer (**Rasa & Rakta Dhatu**).

## Causes of Canine Skin Irritation

- **Pitta Heat Elevation:** Internal heat excess causing hot spots, bright red rashes, and irritability.
- **Environmental Allergens:** Pollen, dust mites, lawn chemicals triggering immune flare-ups.
- **Dietary Sensitivity:** Processed kibble containing inflammatory fillers or rendered proteins.

## Top Ayurvedic Topical Remedies

### 1. Neem Leaf Water Wash (*Nimba*)
Neem is nature's most potent bitter disinfectant (**Katu/Tikta**). Boiled and cooled Neem tea makes an incredible soothing coat rinse to eliminate bacteria, fungi, and itch-causing parasites.

### 2. Aloe Vera Gel & Coconut Oil Blend
Fresh pure Aloe Vera gel mixed with cold-pressed coconut oil provides instant cooling relief to inflamed hot spots and red bellies.

### 3. Herbal Oat & Turmeric Bath
Ground colloidal oatmeal mixed with a pinch of turmeric powder absorbs skin toxins while restoring the lipid barrier.
