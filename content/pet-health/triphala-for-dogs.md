# Triphala for Dogs: Digestive Health & Natural Detox Guide

> **Clinical Executive Summary**: This guide on **Triphala for Dogs: Digestive Health & Natural Detox Guide** synthesizes classical Sanskrit Samhita principles with modern botanical pharmacological research. Curated under the editorial supervision of Suresh Bhati, it provides actionable doshic guidelines, evidence-based applications, and safety parameters.

Triphala—the classic Ayurvedic formula composed of three revered fruits (**Amalaki**, **Haritaki**, and **Bibhitaki**)—is one of the safest and most effective botanical remedies for canine digestive health.

---

## Why Triphala Works for Canine Digestion

1. **Tridoshic Balance:** Harmonizes Vata (colon regulation), Pitta (liver & bile flow), and Kapha (mucus clearance).
2. **Gentle Bowel Cleansing:** Encourages natural peristalsis without harsh laxative spasms.
3. **Antioxidant Powerhouse:** Amalaki provides natural Vitamin C bioflavonoids that strengthen systemic immunity.
4. **Gut Microbiome Support:** Promotes beneficial intestinal flora and clears metabolic toxins (**Ama**).

---

## Indications for Canine Use

- Occasional constipation or hard stools.
- Bad breath (*halitosis*) originating from digestive congestion.
- Dull, greasy coat or skin breakouts.
- Sluggish digestion in senior dogs.

---

## Safe Dosage & Administration

- **Micro-Dosage Rule:** Always start with a small pinch added to wet food.
- **Small Dogs:** 50 mg to 100 mg daily.
- **Medium Dogs:** 100 mg to 250 mg daily.
- **Large Dogs:** 250 mg to 500 mg daily.

*Note: Ensure fresh water is available at all times. Do not administer during acute watery diarrhea.*
