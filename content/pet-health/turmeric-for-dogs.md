# Turmeric for Dogs: Benefits, Dosage & Safety Guide

Turmeric (**Haridra** in Sanskrit) is one of the most widely researched herbs in both modern pharmacology and ancient Ayurvedic medicine. For dogs, curcumin (the active polyphenol in turmeric) provides exceptional support for joint flexibility, cardiovascular wellness, and hepatic detoxification.

---

## Health Benefits for Canines

1. **Natural Joint Relief:** Curcumin inhibits inflammatory enzymes (COX-2 and iNOS), easing stiffness in senior dogs with arthritis.
2. **Liver Support:** Stimulates bile flow (**Pitta**) and aids hepatic enzymes in breaking down toxins.
3. **Cardiovascular & Immunity Support:** Potent antioxidant properties neutralize free radicals and promote healthy cell longevity.

---

## How to Make Ayurvedic Golden Paste for Dogs

Raw turmeric powder is poorly absorbed by the canine gut unless combined with healthy fats and black pepper.

### Ingredients:
- ½ cup organic turmeric powder (*Haridra Churna*)
- 1 cup filtered water
- ⅓ cup organic cold-pressed coconut oil
- ½ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper (*Pippali / Maricha*)

### Instructions:
1. Simmer water and turmeric powder in a saucepan over low heat for 7–10 minutes until a thick paste forms.
2. Remove from heat, allow to cool slightly, then stir in coconut oil and black pepper.
3. Store in a glass jar in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.

---

## Recommended Daily Dosage of Golden Paste

- **Small Dogs:** ⅛ to ¼ teaspoon daily.
- **Medium Dogs:** ½ teaspoon twice daily.
- **Large Dogs:** 1 teaspoon twice daily.
