# Ashwagandha for Dogs: Benefits, Dosage & Safety Guide

Ashwagandha (**Withania somnifera**), known in Sanskrit as the "Strength of a Horse," is the premier Rasayana (rejuvenative) herb in Ayurvedic veterinary care.

---

## Key Benefits for Dogs

1. **Cortisol & Stress Regulation:** Modulates adrenal output during environmental changes, loud storms, or travel.
2. **Muscle & Joint Strength:** Supports muscular tone in aging dogs experiencing hind leg weakness.
3. **Immune System Modulation:** Rebalances overactive immune responses during seasonal shifts.

---

## Safe Dosage & Guidelines

- **Small Dogs (under 15 lbs):** 50 mg to 100 mg daily.
- **Medium Dogs (15–45 lbs):** 150 mg to 250 mg daily.
- **Large Dogs (45+ lbs):** 300 mg to 500 mg daily.

*Always choose standardized organic Ashwagandha root powder without added fillers.*
