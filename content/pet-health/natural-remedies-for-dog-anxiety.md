Nighttime anxiety, pacing, whining, and restlessness in dogs are classic signs of a **Vata imbalance** in Ayurvedic veterinary science. Vata energy governs the nervous system, and when elevated by loud noises, aging cognitive changes, or routine disruptions, it results in fear and sleep disturbances.

## Understanding Canine Night Anxiety

Canine nighttime anxiety can stem from multiple factors:
- **Noise Phobia:** Fireworks, thunderstorms, or wind noises.
- **Separation Anxiety:** Fear of isolation when owners go to sleep.
- **Cognitive Dysfunction:** Senior dogs experiencing disorientation as darkness falls (sundowning).
- **Physical Discomfort:** Joint stiffness or digestive discomfort elevating nighttime stress.

Known as the flagship Ayurvedic adaptogen, Ashwagandha modulates cortisol levels and stabilizes the central nervous system without causing heavy sedation.

### 2. Brahmi (*Bacopa monnieri*)
Brahmi rejuvenates nervous tissues, supports cognitive clarity in senior pets, and promotes peaceful, undisturbed sleep cycles.

### 3. Chamomile & Valerian Root Rinses
Mild herbal chamomile tea (cooled) added to evening food helps soothe the digestive fire (**Agni**) and calm nervous muscle tension.

## Environmental & Tactile Vata-Balancing Techniques

1. **Warm Massage (Abhyanga):** Gently massage your dog's ears, forehead, and back with warm organic sesame or coconut oil 30 minutes before bedtime.
2. **Weighted Blanket or Pressure Vest:** Deep touch pressure provides grounding sensation that pacifies erratic Vata energy.
3. **Calming Soundscapes:** Play soft classical music or ambient white noise to block sudden external disturbance.
