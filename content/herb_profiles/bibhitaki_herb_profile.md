# Bibhitaki (Terminalia Bellirica) — Botanical Profile & Triphala Constituent #3

Bibhitaki (बिभीतकी) in Sanskrit signifies *"That Which Keeps You Away From Disease"*. It is the Kapha-pacifying member of the **[Triphala](/articles/triphala_herb_profile)** formulation, celebrated for its unique ability to liquefy stubborn lung mucus, dry excess metabolic dampness, and clear visceral fat.

## 🌬️ 2. Ayurvedic Energy Profile (Taseer & Dravyaguna)

- **Rasa (Taste):** *Kashaya* (Astringent)
- **Guna (Qualities):** *Ruksha* (Dry), *Laghu* (Light)
- **Virya (Taseer / Potency):** **Usna (Warm / Drying Taseer)** — Dissolves accumulated Kapha dampness in lungs and liver.
- **Vipaka (Post-Digestive Effect):** *Madhura* (Sweet)
- **Dosha Karma:** **Kapha Shamak** (Clears phlegm, congestion, and fat deposits).

---

## 🧪 3. Phytochemical & Triphala Synergy

- **Bellericanin & Lignans:** Possesses potent mucolytic and bronchodilator actions.
- **Gallic & Ellagic Acid:** Inhibits bacterial adhesion on respiratory mucosal linings.
- **Role in [Triphala](/articles/triphala_herb_profile):** Forms 33.3% of Triphala, balancing Kapha alongside [Amalaki](/articles/amalaki_herb_profile) (Cooling Pitta) and [Haritaki](/articles/haritaki_herb_profile) (Warming Vata).

---

## 💡 4. Primary Clinical Use Cases

1. **Bronchial & Lung Phlegm Relief:** Liquefies sticky bronchial mucus in cough, asthma, and sinusitis.
2. **Lipid & Cholesterol Management:** Reduces blood triglycerides and fatty liver accumulation.
3. **Voice Quality & Throat Health:** Soothes vocal cord hoarseness in singers and public speakers.

---

## 💊 5. Dosage & Classical Formulations

- **Bibhitaki Churna:** 2g – 4g twice daily with honey or warm water.
- **Part of [Triphala Churna](/articles/triphala_herb_profile):** 3g – 6g taken at bedtime.
