# Haritaki (Terminalia Chebula) — Botanical Profile & Triphala Constituent #2

Haritaki (हरीतकी) is hailed in ancient Sanskrit literature as the *"King of Medicines"* and *"Mother of Herbs"*. It is the Vata-pacifying constituent of the **[Triphala](/articles/triphala_herb_profile)** triad, renowned for promoting intestinal motility, brain function, and systemic detox.

## 🔥 2. Ayurvedic Energy Profile (Taseer & Dravyaguna)

- **Rasa (Taste):** All tastes except salty — predominantly *Kashaya* (Astringent) and *Tikta* (Bitter).
- **Guna (Qualities):** *Laghu* (Light), *Ruksha* (Dry)
- **Virya (Taseer / Potency):** **Usna (Warm / Mildly Hot Taseer)** — Stimulates sluggish intestinal motility and clears cold Vata gas.
- **Vipaka (Post-Digestive Effect):** *Madhura* (Sweet)
- **Dosha Karma:** **Vata Shamak** (Promotes downward movement of intestinal Vata — *Anulomana*).

---

## 🧪 3. Phytochemical & Triphala Synergy

- **Chebulinic Acid & Tannins:** Promotes smooth muscle relaxation in colon walls, preventing constipation.
- **Corilagin & Ellagic Acid:** Provides broad-spectrum antioxidant and liver protection.
- **Role in [Triphala](/articles/triphala_herb_profile):** Forms 33.3% of Triphala, balancing Vata alongside [Amalaki](/articles/amalaki_herb_profile) (Cooling Pitta) and [Bibhitaki](/articles/bibhitaki_herb_profile) (Drying Kapha).

---

## 💡 4. Primary Clinical Use Cases

1. **Chronic Constipation & Bloating:** Restores natural rhythmic contractions (*Peristalsis*) without irritation.
2. **Cognitive Function & Brain Health:** Acts as a Medhya Rasayana to enhance focus and memory.
3. **Oral Health & Wound Healing:** Gargling Haritaki decoction heals mouth ulcers and bleeding gums.

---

## 💊 5. Dosage & Classical Formulations

- **Haritaki Churna:** 3g – 5g at bedtime with warm water.
- **Abhayarishta:** Fermented classical tonic for digestive wellness and hemorrhoidal support.
- **Part of [Triphala Churna](/articles/triphala_herb_profile):** 3g – 6g daily.
