# Turmeric / Haridra (Curcuma Longa) — Complete Botanical Profile

Turmeric (हरिद्रा) is known in Sanskrit as **Kanchani** (*Golden Goddess*). It is the premier anti-inflammatory, blood purifier, and skin rejuvenator across traditional Indian medicine.

---

## 🌿 1. Botanical & Multilingual Nomenclature

| Parameter | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Botanical Name** | *Curcuma longa* L. |
| **Family** | Zingiberaceae (Ginger family) |
| **Sanskrit Names** | Haridra (हरिद्रा), Kanchani, Nisha, Krimighni, Haldi |
| **Hindi Name** | Haldi (हल्दी) |
| **English Names** | Turmeric, Golden Spice, Curcuma |
| **Tamil Name** | Manjal (மஞ்சள்) |
| **Telugu Name** | Pasupu (పసుపు) |
| **Arabic Name** | Kurkum (كركم) |
| **Chinese Name** | Jiang Huang (姜黄) |

## 🔥 2. Ayurvedic Energy Profile (Taseer & Dravyaguna)

- **Rasa (Taste):** *Tikta* (Bitter), *Katu* (Pungent)
- **Guna (Qualities):** *Ruksha* (Dry), *Laghu* (Light)
- **Virya (Taseer / Potency):** **Usna (Warm / Hot Taseer)** — Clears stagnant cold blood, damp phlegm, and toxic Ama.
- **Vipaka (Post-Digestive Effect):** *Katu* (Pungent)
- **Dosha Karma:** **Tridosha Shamak** (Dampens Kapha and Vata; purifies blood without provoking Pitta when taken in moderate culinary doses).

---

## 🧪 3. Phytochemical Composition

- **Curcuminoids (Curcumin, Demethoxycurcumin, Bisdemethoxycurcumin):** Polyphenolic compounds that downregulate NF-kB, COX-2, and 5-LOX inflammatory pathways.
- **Turmerones (alpha & beta-turmerone):** Essential oils that enhance brain neurogenesis and curcumin intestinal absorption.
- **Piperine Bio-enhancement:** Combining Turmeric with Black Pepper (*Piper nigrum*) increases Curcumin bioavailability by 2,000%.

---

## 💡 4. Primary Clinical Use Cases

1. **Joint Inflammation & Arthritis (Sandhivata):** Reduces joint pain, morning stiffness, and swelling.
2. **Blood Purification & Skin Radiance:** Clears acne, eczema, and hyperpigmentation by purifying Rakta Dhatu.
3. **Metabolic Health & Fatty Liver:** Prevents lipid accumulation in hepatic tissue.

---

## 💊 5. Dosage & Classical Formulations

- **Haridra Churna:** 1g – 3g with warm milk, black pepper, and Ghee (*Golden Milk*).
- **Haridra Khanda:** Classical herbal formulation for allergic skin hives and respiratory allergies.
