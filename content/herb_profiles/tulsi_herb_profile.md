# Tulsi / Holy Basil (Ocimum Sanctum) — Complete Botanical Profile

Tulsi (तुलसी) is worshiped across India as *"The Incomparable One"* and *"Queen of Herbs"*. It is revered in Ayurveda for its adaptogenic, antimicrobial, and Kapha-Vata balancing power.

---

## 🌿 1. Botanical & Multilingual Nomenclature

| Parameter | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Botanical Name** | *Ocimum sanctum* L. (syn. *Ocimum tenuiflorum*) |
| **Family** | Lamiaceae (Mint family) |
| **Sanskrit Names** | Tulsi (तुलसी), Surasa, Devadundubhi, Manjarika, Vishnupriya |
| **Hindi Name** | Tulsi (तुलसी) |
| **English Names** | Holy Basil, Sacred Basil, Indian Basil |
| **Tamil Name** | Thulasi (துளசி) |
| **Telugu Name** | Tulasi (తులసి) |
| **Arabic Name** | Raihan-e-Hindi (ريحان هندي) |
| **Chinese Name** | Sheng Luo Le (圣罗勒) |

## 🔥 2. Ayurvedic Energy Profile (Taseer & Dravyaguna)

- **Rasa (Taste):** *Katu* (Pungent), *Tikta* (Bitter)
- **Guna (Qualities):** *Laghu* (Light), *Ruksha* (Dry)
- **Virya (Taseer / Potency):** **Usna (Warm / Hot Taseer)** — Expels cold dampness from chest and sinuses.
- **Vipaka (Post-Digestive Effect):** *Katu* (Pungent)
- **Dosha Karma:** **Kapha-Vata Shamak** (Dampens excessive lung phlegm and cold chills; may mildly elevate Pitta if overconsumed).

---

## 🧪 3. Phytochemical Composition

- **Eugenol (1-hydroxy-2-methoxy-4-allylbenzene):** Primary active essential oil component exhibiting strong antiviral, antibacterial, and analgesic actions.
- **Ursolic Acid & Rosmarinic Acid:** Potent anti-inflammatory antioxidants that reduce histamine release.
- **Caryophyllene:** Natural cannabinoid receptor (CB2) binder supporting immune response.

---

## 💡 4. Primary Clinical Use Cases

1. **Respiratory & Bronchial Clearance:** Clears throat congestion, asthma, bronchitis, and seasonal allergic rhinitis.
2. **Adaptogenic Stress Resistance:** Regulates cortisol levels and mental fatigue.
3. **Blood Sugar & Metabolic Control:** Enhances pancreatic beta-cell function.

---

## 💊 5. Dosage & Classical Formulations

- **Fresh Tulsi Leaves:** 5 to 10 fresh leaves chewed daily on an empty stomach.
- **Tulsi Swaras (Juice):** 5ml – 10ml with honey.
- **Tulsi Tea / Kadha:** Decoction infused with black pepper and ginger for colds.
