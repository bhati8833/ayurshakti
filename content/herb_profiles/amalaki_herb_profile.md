# Amalaki / Amla (Phyllanthus Emblica) — Botanical Profile & Triphala Constituent #1

Amalaki (अमलकी), commonly known as **Amla** or Indian Gooseberry, is the chief constituent of the famous **[Triphala](/articles/triphala_herb_profile)** formulation. It is considered one of the highest natural sources of stable Vitamin C and the premier cooling Rasayana in Ayurveda.

## ❄️ 2. Ayurvedic Energy Profile (Taseer & Dravyaguna)

- **Rasa (Taste):** Contains 5 tastes — predominantly *Amla* (Sour), followed by *Madhura* (Sweet), *Tikta* (Bitter), *Kashaya* (Astringent), and *Katu* (Pungent).
- **Guna (Qualities):** *Laghu* (Light), *Ruksha* (Dry)
- **Virya (Taseer / Potency):** **Sheeta (Cold / Cooling Taseer)** — Uniquely, despite being sour in taste, Amla has a cooling virya that calms Pitta rather than aggravating it.
- **Vipaka (Post-Digestive Effect):** *Madhura* (Sweet)
- **Dosha Karma:** **Pitta Shamak** (Dampens hyperacidity, heat, and internal inflammation).

---

## 🧪 3. Phytochemical & Triphala Synergy

- **Vitamin C Complex:** Contains 600mg to 700mg of Vitamin C per 100g of fresh fruit juice.
- **Emblicanin A & B:** Tannins that encapsulate Vitamin C molecules, protecting them from heat degradation during storage.
- **Role in [Triphala](/articles/triphala_herb_profile):** Forms 33.3% of the Triphala matrix, providing the cooling Pitta-cleansing component alongside [Haritaki](/articles/haritaki_herb_profile) (Warming Vata) and [Bibhitaki](/articles/bibhitaki_herb_profile) (Drying Kapha).

---

## 💡 4. Primary Clinical Use Cases

1. **Hyperacidity & Acid Reflux (Amlapitta):** Neutralizes gastric hydrochloric acid without causing rebound secretion.
2. **Hair & Skin Longevity:** Boosts collagen synthesis and inhibits 5-alpha reductase to prevent hair loss.
3. **Glycemic & Lipid Management:** Improves endothelial function and reduces LDL oxidation.

---

## 💊 5. Dosage & Classical Formulations

- **Fresh Amla Juice:** 10ml – 20ml daily.
- **Chyawanprash:** Master Ayurvedic herbal jam made with raw fresh Amla base.
- **Part of [Triphala Churna](/articles/triphala_herb_profile):** 3g – 6g at bedtime.
