# Giloy / Guduchi (Tinospora Cordifolia) — Complete Botanical Profile

![Giloy Guduchi Botanical Profile](/images/herbs/giloy.png)

Giloy is revered in classical Sanskrit texts as **Amrita** (Divine Nectar of Immortality). It possesses the rare Ayurvedic virtue of **Tridosha Shamak** — harmonizing Vata, Pitta, and Kapha simultaneously without causing metabolic imbalance.

---

## 🌿 1. Botanical & Multilingual Nomenclature

| Parameter | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Botanical Name** | *Tinospora cordifolia* (Willd.) Miers |
| **Family** | Menispermaceae (Moonseed family) |
| **Sanskrit Names** | Guduchi (गुडूची), Amrita, Chhinnaruha, Vayastha, Vishalya |
| **Hindi Name** | Giloy (गिलोय), Gurach, Gurcha |
| **English Names** | Heart-leaved Moonseed, Indian Tinospora, Heavenly Elixir |
| **Tamil Name** | Seenthil Kodi (சீந்தில் கொடி) |
| **Telugu Name** | Tippa Teega (తిప్ప తీగ) |
| **Arabic Name** | Gilo-e-Hindi (جلو هندي) |
| **Chinese Name** | Ku Mu Teng (苦木藤) |

## ⚖️ 2. Ayurvedic Energy Profile (Taseer & Dravyaguna)

- **Rasa (Taste):** *Tikta* (Bitter), *Kashaya* (Astringent)
- **Guna (Qualities):** *Laghu* (Light), *Snigdha* (Unctuous)
- **Virya (Taseer / Potency):** **Usna (Warm Taseer with Neutralizing Action)** — Burns toxic Ama without provoking Pitta inflammation.
- **Vipaka (Post-Digestive Effect):** *Madhura* (Sweet) — Rebuilds immune white blood cells (Ojas).
- **Dosha Karma (Dosha Impact):** 
  - **Tridoshahara:** Balances Vata, Pitta, and Kapha equally.
  - **Jwarahara:** Premier classical herb for chronic fevers and post-viral fatigue.

---

## 🧪 3. Phytochemical & Nutritional Composition

- **Tinosporine & Cordifoliosides:** Diterpenoid lactones that stimulate macrophage engulfment of pathogens.
- **Berberine & Magnoflorine:** Natural anti-microbial and blood-purifying alkaloids.
- **Arabinogalactan Polysaccharides:** Modulates cytokine response and immune defense.
- **Zinc & Iron:** Supports healthy platelet counts during fevers.

---

## 💡 4. Primary Clinical Use Cases

1. **Immune Modulation & Fever Relief:** Clears lingering post-viral inflammation and maintains healthy body temperature.
2. **Liver Detoxification & Jaundice:** Scavenges hepatic toxins and regenerates liver cell membranes.
3. **Glycemic Control:** Enhances insulin sensitivity and cellular glucose uptake.
4. **Gout & Uric Acid Control (Vatarakta):** Excretes excess blood uric acid crystals.

---

## 💊 5. Dosage & Classical Formulations

- **Guduchi Satva (Pure Starch Extract):** 500mg – 1g twice daily with honey or warm water.
- **Giloy Swaras (Fresh Juice):** 10ml – 20ml diluted in equal water on an empty stomach.
- **Sudarshan Ghanvati:** Classical compound tablet containing Giloy for pyrexia.
