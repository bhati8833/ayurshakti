# Cikitsasthana (Cikitsa Sthana) — Section on Therapeutics

## TL;DR — Executive Clinical Summary

This chapter from **Charaka Samhita (Chikitsasthana)** addresses the foundational principles of traditional Indian internal medicine (*Kāyacikitsā*). Composed originally by **Acharya Agnivesha** and revised by **Acharya Charaka**, this section provides evidence-based dietary, physiological, and therapeutic recommendations for balancing the three doshas (*Vata*, *Pitta*, and *Kapha*).
