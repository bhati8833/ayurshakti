# Chapter 2 - The Seeds of Rough chaff (apamarga-tanduliya)

## TL;DR — Executive Clinical Summary

**The Seeds of Rough chaff** — This chapter of the Charaka Samhita, Kalpasthana Chapter 2, expounds the seeds of rough chaff (apamarga-tanduliya) as taught by Acharya Atreya through Agnivesha and revised by Acharya Charaka. It covers the pharmaceutical preparations (kalpa) of purificatory herbs and their methods of use.
