# Chapter 3 - The Pathology of Gulma (gulma-nidana)

## TL;DR — Executive Clinical Summary

**The Pathology of Gulma** — This chapter of the Charaka Samhita, Nidanasthana Chapter 3, expounds the pathology of gulma (gulma-nidana) as taught by Acharya Atreya through Agnivesha and revised by Acharya Charaka. It covers the etiology (nidana), premonitory symptoms (purvarupa), and clinical varieties of disease.
