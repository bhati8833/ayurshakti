## ❓ 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is Triphala (Triphala (polyherbal formulation))?

**A:** Triphala is a classical Ayurvedic polyherbal formulation traditionally used in polyherbal. It combines multiple herbs for synergistic therapeutic effects.

### Q2: What are the ingredients in Triphala?

**A:** Triphala contains: amalaki, haritaki, bibhitaki. These herbs work synergistically for enhanced therapeutic benefit.

### Q3: What are the benefits of Triphala?

**A:** Traditionally used for polyherbal. It helps balance Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas and supports overall wellness.

### Q4: How to take Triphala?

**A:** Dosage varies by individual constitution and condition. Typically taken as churna (powder), kwath (decoction), or tablet form. Consult an Ayurvedic practitioner for personalized dosage.

### Q5: Are there any side effects of Triphala?

**A:** Generally safe when taken as directed. May cause mild digestive upset in sensitive individuals. Pregnant/nursing women and those on medications should consult a healthcare provider before use.
