# Guduchi (Tinospora cordifolia)

> **TL;DR:** Tinospora cordifolia (Guduchi) is a Menispermaceae herb traditionally used for **immune modulation & fever relief:** clears lingering post-viral inflammation and maintains healthy body temperature.. It works by balancing Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas. Always consult a qualified Ayurvedic practitioner before use.

## ❓ 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is Guduchi (Tinospora cordifolia)?

**A:** Guduchi is a Menispermaceae herb in classical Ayurvedic medicine. It is valued for its **immune modulation & fever relief:** clears lingering post-viral inflammation and maintains healthy body temperature. properties and ability to balance doshas.

### Q2: What are the benefits of Guduchi?

**A:** Traditionally used for **immune modulation & fever relief:** clears lingering post-viral inflammation and maintains healthy body temperature.. It helps balance Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas. **Immune Modulation & Fever Relief:** Clears lingering post-viral inflammation and maintains healthy body temperature.. **Liver Detoxification & Jaundice:** Scavenges hepatic toxins and regenerates liver cell membranes.

### Q3: How to use Guduchi?

**A:** Available as powder (churna), decoction (kwath), tablet, or capsule. Typical dosage: 3-6g powder or 15-30ml decoction twice daily with warm water or as directed by practitioner.

### Q4: Can Guduchi be taken daily?

**A:** Yes, many Ayurvedic herbs are taken daily as rasayana (rejuvenative). However, cycling (e.g., 6 weeks on, 2 weeks off) is often recommended. Consult an Ayurvedic practitioner for your constitution.

### Q5: Any side effects or interactions of Guduchi?

**A:** Generally safe at recommended doses. May interact with certain medications (blood thinners, diabetes meds, sedatives). Pregnant/nursing women and those with medical conditions should consult a healthcare provider before use.
