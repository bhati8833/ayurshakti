# Haridra (Curcuma longa)

> **TL;DR:** Curcuma longa (Haridra) is a Zingiberaceae herb traditionally used for **joint inflammation & arthritis (sandhivata):** reduces joint pain, morning stiffness, and swelling.. It works by balancing Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas. Always consult a qualified Ayurvedic practitioner before use.

## ❓ 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is Haridra (Curcuma longa)?

**A:** Haridra is a Zingiberaceae herb in classical Ayurvedic medicine. It is valued for its **joint inflammation & arthritis (sandhivata):** reduces joint pain, morning stiffness, and swelling. properties and ability to balance doshas.

### Q2: What are the benefits of Haridra?

**A:** Traditionally used for **joint inflammation & arthritis (sandhivata):** reduces joint pain, morning stiffness, and swelling.. It helps balance Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas. **Joint Inflammation & Arthritis (Sandhivata):** Reduces joint pain, morning stiffness, and swelling.. **Blood Purification & Skin Radiance:** Clears acne, eczema, and hyperpigmentation by purifying Rakta Dhatu.

### Q3: How to use Haridra?

**A:** Available as powder (churna), decoction (kwath), tablet, or capsule. Typical dosage: 3-6g powder or 15-30ml decoction twice daily with warm water or as directed by practitioner.

### Q4: Can Haridra be taken daily?

**A:** Yes, many Ayurvedic herbs are taken daily as rasayana (rejuvenative). However, cycling (e.g., 6 weeks on, 2 weeks off) is often recommended. Consult an Ayurvedic practitioner for your constitution.

### Q5: Any side effects or interactions of Haridra?

**A:** Generally safe at recommended doses. May interact with certain medications (blood thinners, diabetes meds, sedatives). Pregnant/nursing women and those with medical conditions should consult a healthcare provider before use.
