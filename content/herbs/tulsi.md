# Tulsi (Ocimum sanctum)

> **TL;DR:** Ocimum sanctum (Tulsi) is a Lamiaceae herb traditionally used for **respiratory & bronchial clearance:** clears throat congestion, asthma, bronchitis, and seasonal allergic rhinitis.. It works by balancing Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas. Always consult a qualified Ayurvedic practitioner before use.

## ❓ 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is Tulsi (Ocimum sanctum)?

**A:** Tulsi is a Lamiaceae herb in classical Ayurvedic medicine. It is valued for its **respiratory & bronchial clearance:** clears throat congestion, asthma, bronchitis, and seasonal allergic rhinitis. properties and ability to balance doshas.

### Q2: What are the benefits of Tulsi?

**A:** Traditionally used for **respiratory & bronchial clearance:** clears throat congestion, asthma, bronchitis, and seasonal allergic rhinitis.. It helps balance Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas. **Respiratory & Bronchial Clearance:** Clears throat congestion, asthma, bronchitis, and seasonal allergic rhinitis.. **Adaptogenic Stress Resistance:** Regulates cortisol levels and mental fatigue.

### Q3: How to use Tulsi?

**A:** Available as powder (churna), decoction (kwath), tablet, or capsule. Typical dosage: 3-6g powder or 15-30ml decoction twice daily with warm water or as directed by practitioner.

### Q4: Can Tulsi be taken daily?

**A:** Yes, many Ayurvedic herbs are taken daily as rasayana (rejuvenative). However, cycling (e.g., 6 weeks on, 2 weeks off) is often recommended. Consult an Ayurvedic practitioner for your constitution.

### Q5: Any side effects or interactions of Tulsi?

**A:** Generally safe at recommended doses. May interact with certain medications (blood thinners, diabetes meds, sedatives). Pregnant/nursing women and those with medical conditions should consult a healthcare provider before use.
