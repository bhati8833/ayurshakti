### Q1: What is Shatavari (Asparagus racemosus)?

**A:** Shatavari is a Asparagaceae herb in classical Ayurvedic medicine. It is valued for its **female hormonal harmony:** balances estrogen fluctuations during pms, pcos, perimenopause, and menopause. properties and ability to balance doshas.

### Q2: What are the benefits of Shatavari?

**A:** Traditionally used for **female hormonal harmony:** balances estrogen fluctuations during pms, pcos, perimenopause, and menopause.. It helps balance Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas. **Female Hormonal Harmony:** Balances estrogen fluctuations during PMS, PCOS, perimenopause, and menopause.. **Galactagogue Effect:** Promotes prolactin secretion, enhancing breast milk supply in lactating mothers.

### Q3: How to use Shatavari?

**A:** Available as powder (churna), decoction (kwath), tablet, or capsule. Typical dosage: 3-6g powder or 15-30ml decoction twice daily with warm water or as directed by practitioner.

### Q4: Can Shatavari be taken daily?

**A:** Yes, many Ayurvedic herbs are taken daily as rasayana (rejuvenative). However, cycling (e.g., 6 weeks on, 2 weeks off) is often recommended. Consult an Ayurvedic practitioner for your constitution.

### Q5: Any side effects or interactions of Shatavari?

**A:** Generally safe at recommended doses. May interact with certain medications (blood thinners, diabetes meds, sedatives). Pregnant/nursing women and those with medical conditions should consult a healthcare provider before use.
