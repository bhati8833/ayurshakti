# Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera)

> **TL;DR:** Withania somnifera (Ashwagandha) is a Solanaceae herb traditionally used for **hpa axis & cortisol modulation:** lowers serum cortisol levels by up to 27.9%, attenuating chronic stress and anxiety.. It works by balancing Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas. Always consult a qualified Ayurvedic practitioner before use.

## 🔥 2. Ayurvedic Energy Profile (Taseer & Dravyaguna)

- **Rasa (Taste):** Tikta
- **Guna (Qualities):** Laghu
- **Virya (Taseer / Potency):** Usna (Warm / Hot Taseer)
- **Vipaka (Post-Digestive Effect):** Madhura
- **Dosha Karma (Dosha Impact):** - **Vata Shamak:** Strongly pacifies nervous Vata imbalance.
  - **Kapha Shamak:** Reduces excess sluggish Kapha.
  - *Note:* Excessive hot-potency use may mildly elevate Pitta in hot climates.

## 💡 4. Primary Clinical Use Cases

1. **HPA Axis & Cortisol Modulation:** Lowers serum cortisol levels by up to 27.9%, attenuating chronic stress and anxiety.
2. **Reproductive & Muscle Vitality:** Enhances testosterone production, sperm motility in men, and stamina in athletic recovery.
3. **Neuroprotection & Sleep Improvement:** Somnifera translates to "sleep-inducing". It enhances GABAergic signaling for deep sleep without sedation.
4. **Thyroid Function:** Supports T3 and T4 hormone synthesis in subclinical hypothyroidism.

## ❓ 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera)?

**A:** Ashwagandha is a Solanaceae herb in classical Ayurvedic medicine. It is valued for its **hpa axis & cortisol modulation:** lowers serum cortisol levels by up to 27.9%, attenuating chronic stress and anxiety. properties and ability to balance doshas.

### Q2: What are the benefits of Ashwagandha?

**A:** Traditionally used for **hpa axis & cortisol modulation:** lowers serum cortisol levels by up to 27.9%, attenuating chronic stress and anxiety.. It helps balance Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas. **HPA Axis & Cortisol Modulation:** Lowers serum cortisol levels by up to 27.9%, attenuating chronic stress and anxiety.. **Reproductive & Muscle Vitality:** Enhances testosterone production, sperm motility in men, and stamina in athletic recovery.

### Q3: How to use Ashwagandha?

**A:** Available as powder (churna), decoction (kwath), tablet, or capsule. Typical dosage: 3-6g powder or 15-30ml decoction twice daily with warm water or as directed by practitioner.

### Q4: Can Ashwagandha be taken daily?

**A:** Yes, many Ayurvedic herbs are taken daily as rasayana (rejuvenative). However, cycling (e.g., 6 weeks on, 2 weeks off) is often recommended. Consult an Ayurvedic practitioner for your constitution.

### Q5: Any side effects or interactions of Ashwagandha?

**A:** Generally safe at recommended doses. May interact with certain medications (blood thinners, diabetes meds, sedatives). Pregnant/nursing women and those with medical conditions should consult a healthcare provider before use.
