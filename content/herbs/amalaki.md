# Amalaki (Phyllanthus emblica)

> **TL;DR:** Phyllanthus emblica (Amalaki) is a Phyllanthaceae herb traditionally used for **hyperacidity & acid reflux (amlapitta):** neutralizes gastric hydrochloric acid without causing rebound secretion.. It works by balancing Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas. Always consult a qualified Ayurvedic practitioner before use.

## ❓ 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is Amalaki (Phyllanthus emblica)?

**A:** Amalaki is a Phyllanthaceae herb in classical Ayurvedic medicine. It is also a key component of the Triphala formulation.

### Q2: What are the main benefits of Amalaki?

**A:** Traditionally used for **hyperacidity & acid reflux (amlapitta):** neutralizes gastric hydrochloric acid without causing rebound secretion.. Supports overall health and dosha balance.

### Q3: How is Amalaki used in Triphala?

**A:** In Triphala, Amalaki works synergistically with other herbs to enhance the formulation's therapeutic effect on phyllanthaceae.

### Q4: How to take Amalaki?

**A:** Available as churna (powder), kwath (decoction), or capsule. Typical dosage: 3-6g powder or 15-30ml decoction twice daily. Consult practitioner for personalized guidance.

### Q5: Any precautions for Amalaki?

**A:** Generally safe. Pregnant/nursing women, those with medical conditions, or on medications should consult an Ayurvedic practitioner before use.
